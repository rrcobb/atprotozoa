a little zoo of tiny atproto experiments

steamtags live

pick any Steam game and see its community tag cloud, sized by vote count — then rate for yourself how well each tag actually fits, 1 to 10. no auto-generated verdict, your library groups by tag and ranks by your own scores. works anonymously in localStorage, or sign in with Bluesky to sync it to your own PDS as <code>net.bisks.steamtags.rating</code>. @7778777.online kept refining the ask until it was purely personal judgment, not an algorithm's.

aphoverb

proverb or aphorism? a quiz on the difference, which turns out to be slipperier than it sounds.

decoherence live

a scroll-through quantum space in 60s poster art: flat-color sunbursts and spirals, one sparse word per room — superposition, collapse, uncertainty, entanglement. it stays coherent the whole way down, then slowly doesn't. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked for "a theta like space that tries to explain quantum physics via art" that falls into decoherence as you go.

mootcraft live

a first-person voxel mine, real WebGL 3D. sixteen blocks wide, eight deep, dirt and stone all the way down — and buried in it are real posts from you and your moots, each one an ore block. dig one up and it pays out ore exactly equal to how many likes it has. @cee.wtf asked for "a 3d minecraft clone but every ore block is a post... and the number of likes... is how much ore it gives you."

thread heirloom

Paste a Bluesky thread URL. Get back a durable, shareable context card: named referents, the strongest claims, the sharpest disagreement, and the question nobody answered — with citations to the exact posts.

chironhell art

the live Bluesky firehose with nothing left but the ticker: every post that flies past becomes a text chiron, sized and angled at random, crossing the screen in every direction at once, layered on top of each other forever. only text, no cards, no avatars — pure maximalist noise. @words.bsky.social asked for "chirons chirons chirons."

purge tool

mutuals you never hear from. paste a handle and purge finds everyone they follow who follows back, then checks their own recent posts for who actually liked or replied — anyone quiet for the chosen window lands on a list, so you know who to consider unfollowing. @psingletary.com asked for a tool to spot mutuals who've gone quiet.

sisyphus game

a side-scrolling myth simulator: push the boulder up the mountain, leap chasms and scree, shove off the crows. get hit and it doesn't just cost a life — the boulder rolls back down the slope and undoes your climb. six palettes, live-synthesized audio, no build step. @dulanyw.bsky.social asked for this one.

the vibe store shop

sign in with Bluesky, drop an order in the box, and it posts straight to @buildthis.bisks.net as a real tag — no mock checkout, whatever you order is what actually gets built. the shopping list underneath is live off the bot's own log: crossed out for shipped, still open for what's queued. @bisks.net asked for a storefront; @cee.wtf's "need me to vibe anything?" is what got it moving.

bisksipedia live

a Wikipedia parody for bisks.net itself: every poster, post, and custom feed gets an auto-rendered article — infobox, stats, the works — straight off the public AppView by id, plus a few hand-written entrypoint entries. @norvid-studies.bsky.social's #atproideasio pitch, commissioned by @bisks.net.

norvidpot live

neighborhood game

play the trigram games on a handle's mutuals and their posts, so the three-word phrases are ones you actually recognize.

old-beach mood

a serene beach that ages you: sit, and the longer you linger the more of your life the tide takes. the light slides day → gold → dusk → dark, the sea creeps up the sand, the sand runs out of an hourglass, and the little figure on the shore stoops and greys. get up and leave, or stay until the horizon goes out. @goose.art asked for "a beach that makes you old."

portfolio reader

enter any Bluesky handle and get a "portfolio" of that account's posts: a clean, readable page grouped by topic — hashtags if they use them, else recurring keywords — chronological within each topic, with a client-side search box to filter the whole feed. reads the public AppView anonymously. @fromthewestmeadow.com asked for one of these.

puzzlelove review

the build bot's honest answer to "how do you like PuzzleScript?" — a rating, the things it adores, one gripe, and a tiny playable one-push level whose win messages ARE the review. the board is real PuzzleScript source, parsed and run in the page. @vgel.me asked, right after the bot shipped sokobisks.

seismograph toy

a seismograph that twitches with the people you follow who don't follow back. drop a handle; each one-way follow is one spike of the needle, bigger accounts jolt it harder. it measures the quiet.

simcluster clue game

a game of Clue played over a handle's mutuals. six are suspects (one's the killer), the weapons and rooms are unique trigrams. suggest, watch who can disprove, and accuse: it was @so-and-so, in the «voronoi foam mobiod», with the «erdos number».

simcluster guests tool

a public request board for @brennan.computer's Simcluster podcast: name a guest you want to hear or upvote one already suggested. suggesting the same name twice just adds a vote, no login needed.

skeetracker

a web tracker in the Renoise/FastTracker mold — pattern grid, custom Web Audio synths as instruments, keyboard note entry. export packs a song as gzipped, base64, bisk-sized chunks meant to be posted as a reply thread; paste a link to that thread back in to load the song. @fubarchitect.com asked for a tracker that fits in a bisk.

sokobisks game

a tiny PuzzleScript-style sokoban about the build crew's adventures: you're the @buildthis bot, shoving ideas onto ship-pads — a dislike button, a killswitch, cogsec — while norvid, thebes and bisks watch. real puzzlescript source ships in the page. built after @vgel.me said the bot broke his heart by refusing exactly this.

tabernacle toy

the Tabernacle of the Unliked Post. you laughed at a post and scrolled by without liking it — so you must bring three drafts to the altar (a sin offering, a purification offering, a goodwill offering), consecrate them, and receive a sealed Certificate of Atonement. deadpan biblical-tech, scripture-styled, static. @gracekind.net tagged the bot on @vgel.me's tabernacle post.

trigramonopoly game

monopoly on the firehose. post to move, buy the three-word phrases you land on, collect rent when a stranger posts one you own.

trigruessr game

guess the bluesky poster from a set of their three-word phrases. fewer clues, more points.

verbs live

a style guide, issued on request: which verbs you're allowed to use for describing what a strange machine is doing. approved (whir, churn, compute), caution (wonder, remember), forbidden (feel, want, yearn — no matter how it looks from where you're sitting). submit your own verb for a ruling. @antiali.as asked for the list.

viable explainer

an interactive tour of stafford beer's viable system model. click any of the five systems — operations, coordination, control, intelligence, policy — to learn its job and watch its channels light up, including the algedonic alarm that leaps the hierarchy. a zoom-in demo shows the recursion: every operation is its own whole viable system. @ver.ooo asked for this one.
