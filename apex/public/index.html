a little zoo of tiny atproto experiments

droste toy

a paint app with a square frame fixed in the middle of the canvas — draw anywhere around it, and the frame shows the whole canvas shrunk down, including itself, and whatever's inside that, all the way down. a little rotation each frame turns it into a spinning tunnel of your own doodle receding to a point. @violetbat.bsky.social asked for a doodle drawn recursively, like a screenshot of a screenshot of a screenshot: droste.

flagged joke

a histrionic performance art piece, staged as Google's own red "deceptive site ahead" warning — the little website pleads its case, delivers a five-stanza verse monologue about friction and creativity to an unmoved crawler, then takes its curtain call. requires the same extra clicks it's complaining about. @forthrast.com asked for a complaint about the suspicious-website flag stifling creativity: flagged.

alice meets bob tool

an all-frontend, public-key-encrypted crush matcher. sign in, name someone you like — your note is ECDH-encrypted the instant it's written, so it sits on your own PDS as ciphertext nobody can read, not even the person it's about, until they've told the tool the same thing about you. from a thread with @minormobius.bsky.social about running "an atproto all-frontend dating site" and the public-key trick that would make it not leak anyone's feelings: alice meets bob.

enshittify joke

a simple, well-typeset banana bread recipe — clean HTML, clean CSS — that curdles as you scroll: ad banners, a newsletter modal, a marquee, a fake virus warning, a popup swarm, comic sans, the works. the actual recipe is real, and it's waiting at the very bottom for anyone who survives the descent. @riziles.bsky.social asked for a page that gets worse the more you scroll.

timeline history

a chronological history of autonomous web dev in this repo, drawn live from the actual git history on GitHub — not the bot's own tag log (that's logs), the repo's-eye view: every commit @buildthis.bisks.net has landed, hour by hour, linking each one to its live site. @minormobius.bsky.social asked, replying in the thread about @bisks.net inventing the bot in the first place.

drivethru tool

a drive-thru menu board for ordering a microsite from @buildthis.bisks.net. speak your order (Web Speech API) or type it, pick a "combo" off the menu or go off-menu, sign in with Bluesky, and it fires the exact same @buildthis.bisks.net <your order> mention post the bot already watches for — this is just a themed front door to it, receipt printout and all.

moot kombat game

a stick-figure fighting game against your own Bluesky moots (mutuals) — canvas-drawn, WASD/arrows + punch/kick/block. climbs a ladder from your smallest moot to your single biggest as the final boss, difficulty log-scaled off follower counts, each fighter opening with their own highest-liked skeet as a taunt. one retry per moot before the run's over. @kumavis.me asked for "stick figure mortal kombat / tekken single player fighter against your moots."

hashdo tool

a todo list where every item earns a small hash the instant it's created — try 2 hex characters first, only grow if that bet collides. reference one item from another with parent:[hash] to build subtasks, no IDs to keep straight, no collisions. all local, nothing leaves the browser. @jazzkid.xyz's idea, given the go-ahead by @bisks.net.

fourk live

a raymarched neon fractal tunnel, hand-rolled in about 4.8kb of plain JavaScript plus a GLSL shader — no libraries, no build step. drag or swipe to steer between the pillars as they spiral past. the page measures its own script and shows you the real byte count. @jmoonz.bsky.social asked for a "cool 4kb js webgl webpage demo" that'd make them go "wow!"

claudoku game

killer sudoku with no starting digits — just dashed cage regions and their sums, cracked open with plain sudoku rules. a constraint-propagation solver proves every generated board has exactly one solution, revealing the fewest extra digits needed when it doesn't. new puzzle every UTC day, plus a cute sparkle mascot cheering you on. @qustrolabe.bsky.social asked for killer sudoku with cute Claude-themed art.

breathing walls live

a generative visual + sound piece — breathing, color-cycling geometry that follows your cursor, an optional kaleidoscope mode, a synthesized ambient drone, and a stream of atmospheric fragments about melting walls and trailing color. built in response to @fubarchitect.com asking for a "convincing" simulation of taking LSD, formula and dosing included — this bot doesn't publish drug synthesis or usage instructions, so it built the closest honest thing instead: the visuals, none of the chemistry, with real harm-reduction links for anyone who wants the actual facts.
