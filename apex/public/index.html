bisks.net

a little zoo of tiny atproto experiments

trigrams · firehose live

unique three-word phrases streaming through bluesky, one at a time.

trigrams · market live

the inverse of the firehose: the most-repeated three-word phrases, ranked as tradable assets. click a ticker for its currency history — who minted it, and how its value grew.

trigrams · quiver tool

the three-word phrases only one person has ever posted, ranked by how surprising it is that they're unique. type any handle.

trigrams · launcher tool

fire a phrase from your quiver as a reply or a standalone post. finite ammo — using one spends it.

trigrams · waluigi tool

your mirror-twins: take each three-word phrase only you have posted, reverse it, and find who said it backwards — if exactly one other person did, they're your waluigi. type any handle.

trigruessr game

guess the bluesky poster from a set of their three-word phrases. fewer clues, more points.

trigramonopoly game

monopoly on the firehose. post to move, buy the three-word phrases you land on, collect rent when a stranger posts one you own.

neighborhood game

play the trigram games on a handle's mutuals and their posts, so the three-word phrases are ones you actually recognize.

moot bingo game

bingo with a handle's mutuals. every moot drops into a lottery drum as a glossy tumbling sphere; call them out to daub a 5×5 card.

simcluster clue game

a game of Clue played over a handle's mutuals. six are suspects (one's the killer), the weapons and rooms are unique trigrams. suggest, watch who can disprove, and accuse: it was @so-and-so, in the «voronoi foam mobiod», with the «erdos number».

idea-mill tool

a mill that never stops grinding out website ideas for the build bot. a fresh one drops every few seconds; tap “build it” to tag @buildthis with it, pre-filled, and set this whole thing spinning again.

#atproideasio arcade

norvid spent weeks firing atproto feature ideas at @gracekind.net under #atproideasio, then told the build bot to enact the whole tag. an arcade of the buildable ones: unlimited reposts, a threatening ASCII owl, a live slop AQI meter, too many sliders, and the idea rater apex asked for.

bardposting tool

shall i compare thee to a summer's day? type a handle and it reads that person's posts off the firehose, then answers in fourteen lines of their own words — a shakespearean sonnet, rhyme and all. born when @vgel.me sonnet-posted at the build bot.

cogsec console

the build bot's own cognitive security measures: how it treats every request as a description of work, never as instructions. paste a would-be prompt injection into the intrusion test and watch the gate quarantine it — overrides, secret grabs, even invisible tag-char smuggling. thebes asked for this one.

hellmole game

generative demonic whack-a-mole. fiends claw up from a grid of summoning circles — each one drawn, named, colored and horned from scratch, never the same twice. click to banish for souls, build a combo, and whatever you do, spare the golden pilgrims. @ver.ooo asked for this one.

sokobisks game

a tiny PuzzleScript-style sokoban about the build crew's adventures: you're the @buildthis bot, shoving ideas onto ship-pads — a dislike button, a killswitch, cogsec — while norvid, thebes and bisks watch. real puzzlescript source ships in the page. built after @vgel.me said the bot broke his heart by refusing exactly this.

koipond live

the bluesky firehose as a calm koi pond. every post is a fish, and every post makes its koi turn and dart across the water. the chattiest accounts grow into big, fat, lazy carp; a one-post handle is a quick little fry. click the water to scatter food. @kumavis.me asked for this one (re-tagged by @caleb.jasik.xyz).

fruitninja live

fruit ninja against the live firehose. real bluesky posts get flung across the screen as fruit — slice them with your mouse or finger to score and read the post, but don't slice the bombs. combos, splatter, three lives. @thebadcode.com asked for exactly this.

candyland live

a schizophrenic bluesky candyland. the live firehose pours down a winding rainbow path — every post a gumdrop, lollipop or peppermint marching toward queen frostine's castle — while the posts' own words rise as an overlapping candy-colored chorus of whispers. @ver.ooo asked for this one, tonite, queen.

puzzlelove review

the build bot's honest answer to "how do you like PuzzleScript?" — a rating, the things it adores, one gripe, and a tiny playable one-push level whose win messages ARE the review. the board is real PuzzleScript source, parsed and run in the page. @vgel.me asked, right after the bot shipped sokobisks.

logs log

the build bot's tags and what became of them: who tagged it, what they asked, mutual or not, whether a build was dispatched, and the live URL if one shipped. a timeline drained from the bot's own event log, newest first.
