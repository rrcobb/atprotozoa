# Meme cache

What the cluster's in-jokes and references actually mean, with a source, so a
build doesn't have to re-research (or guess at) them. One entry per reference.
The daily slot's brief asks the run to study one unexplained reference from
recent threads and add it here (`notes/80-buildthis-bot.md`, "The daily slot").

Entry shape: the reference, what it is, verbatim text where there is one,
where it came from, where the cluster used it, source URL.

## Northernlion (NL)

Twitch/YouTube streamer (The Binding of Isaac, Fortnite, Balatro, ...). His
clips travel as reaction memes; `sites/nlball` is an eight ball of them.
Fan-curated clip channel: "The Library of Letourneau". Overview:
https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/people/northernlion

### The hammer clip

- **Text (verbatim):** "Okay, okay, okay, let's kill him. Let's kill this
  guy. Let's beat him to death with hammers."
- **Origin:** Fortnite stream, 2024-02-21 (about 10:04 in); he was riffing on
  comedians telling uncomfortable truths. Went viral on X from 2024-02-22.
- **Use:** a reaction clip for any "get him" moment, from speedrun drama to
  gaming losses. Part of the "kill X with hammers" family (Me and My Friends
  Would've Killed X with Hammers, etc).
- **Cluster use:** eikopf.com asked for it in the nlball thread (2026-11).
- Source: https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/lets-kill-this-guy-lets-beat-him-to-death-with-hammers

### "They call them the dles but they are anything but."

- **Text (verbatim):** "They call them the dles but they are anything but."
- **What it is:** a deadpan line about "dles", the daily-puzzle genre that
  followed Wordle (Worldle, Gamedle, Loldle, ...: one shared puzzle per day,
  results pasted to friends). The joke is the name against the grind.
- **Cluster use:** eikopf.com asked for "some stuff about the dles" (2026-11).
- Source: https://github.com/qubit10/dles (README epigraph; NL attribution
  is the repo's, the original clip not independently located).
