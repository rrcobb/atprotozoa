karmahose

A running, public, permanent tally for anything at all. No accounts, no targets to register — post something on Bluesky that ends in a name and a delta, and it joins the board.

syntax: end a post's last line with <name> +1, <name> -3, <name>++, or <name>--. <name> is anything — a word, a phrase, a whole sentence. Case doesn't matter and scores stack forever: socialcredit +1 and SocialCredit +1 hit the same tally. Watched live off the public firehose, no login required to add to it.
