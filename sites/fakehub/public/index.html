fakehub

you've seen their avatar. you already know their PRs.

Type a Bluesky handle. fakehub reads the avatar's colors and the last batch of posts, then invents the GitHub profile that goes with it: a contribution graph lit up on the days they actually posted, pinned repos named after their favorite words, PRs with diffs, commit messages, and issue comments. All of it is made up, and all of it happens in your browser.
