how the score works
For every member of the pool, cloutgraph reads their public like records straight off their own PDS (com.atproto.repo.listRecords, collection app.bsky.feed.like — up to 300 recent likes per member) and keeps only the likes aimed at another pool member, giving a directed count c(i,j) = how many times i liked something by j. Each edge is then re-weighted by PPMI: max(0, log(c(i,j)·T / (rowSum(i)·colSum(j)))), T being the total in-pool likes — so an edge only carries weight if the pair like each other more than their individual activity levels would predict by chance. HITS then runs ~100 power-iteration rounds on that weighted graph (authority ← incoming hub·weight, hub ← outgoing authority·weight, L2-normalized each round), and clout is sqrt(hub × authority) per person, scaled 0–100 against the pool's own top scorer. The graph is capped at 50 pool members and 300 likes each to keep the crawl bounded — big mutual sets or lists get truncated to the first 50 found. Any pool member found with fewer than 5 total in-pool likes (given + received) in that initial crawl gets swapped for the next candidate waiting in line — a couple of stray likes is too small a sample for PMI/HITS and tends to swing scores to extremes rather than reflect real standing.
clout = geometric mean of HITS hub & authority scores over a PMI-weighted liker→author like-graph. Reads Bluesky's public AppView (getFollows / getFollowers / getList) plus each pool member's own PDS (listRecords on app.bsky.feed.like) — all anonymous, everything client-side, no login. moots/list pool logic copied from simcluster. sibling of bisks.net.
