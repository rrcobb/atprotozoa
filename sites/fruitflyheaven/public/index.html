A stylized fruit fly connectome — real circuit motifs, not the full 130,000-neuron reconstruction — flying a virtual fly around a garden. Retina bins feed lobula columnar cells and antennal glomeruli, which mix in the mushroom body's Kenyon cells; the fly's actual choices come out of the central complex's heading and goal rings. Every fruit eaten potentiates the mushroom-body pathway for whatever it just smelled and saw — so late in a session the fly beelines for fruit it barely noticed at first.
