Odum's energese, decoded

twelve symbol types. this is deliberate. you were warned.

  • Source — energy entering the system from outside. Here: the sun.
  • Producer — an autotroph converting a flow-in into stored potential. The pond's algae/plants.
  • Consumer — a heterotroph, feeding on stored energy rather than making its own.
  • Apex predator — same hexagon, one trophic level up. Feeds on the consumer, respires its own heat.
  • Decomposer — also a hexagon. Eats detritus, respires heat, and hands recycled nutrients back to storage.
  • Storage — a reservoir. A tank of biomass, nutrients, or detritus building up or draining.
  • Detritus — a second tank, same glyph. Sump for every leak and every leftover in the system.
  • Heat sink — energy dispersed to the environment. Four of them now: the second law's toll, paid four times over.
  • Pathway — a line of flowing energy or material. Weight and motion scale with the flow rate.
  • Leak — the extra pathway this build added. Mass or energy escaping sideways into detritus rather than through a proper sink or a proper predator. Real ponds leak like this; so does this diagram, extravagantly.
  • Interaction — two flows combining to drive a process. There are three of these now, scattered for confusion.
  • System boundary — the dashed line around the whole diagram. The accounting line: what's inside gets counted, what's outside is an import.
  • Emdollar loop — Odum priced real flows in "emdollars." This one's a decoration only; nothing in this simulation runs through it. It is here purely to be one more thing to wonder about.
