🌳 mootree

Sign in with Bluesky and this grows a family tree out of the people you follow. Each row is a generation — how long ago their account was created, relative to yours. Within a row, left-to-right is birth order — the actual order you followed them in, read straight off your repo, oldest follow first.

source reads are all public atproto: your own app.bsky.graph.follow records (for follow order) and app.bsky.actor.getProfiles (for account-creation date). nothing is written anywhere, nothing is stored server-side — the tree is rebuilt in your browser every time you load this page.
