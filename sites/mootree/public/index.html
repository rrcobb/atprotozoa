🌳 mootree

This grows a family tree out of the people someone follows. Rows above yours are a generation each — how long ago that account was created, relative to the tree's owner. Rows below yours are graph-verified lineage: a child has to actually follow you back, a grandchild has to follow that child back, and so on down the tree — an age gap alone doesn't earn a spot. Within a row, left-to-right is birth order — the actual order they followed them in, read straight off their repo, oldest follow first. Every read here is public atproto data, so you can peek at anyone's tree with no sign-in at all — sign in only if you want a permanent link to grow your own.

source reads are all public atproto: your own app.bsky.graph.follow records (for follow order) and app.bsky.actor.getProfiles (for account-creation date). nothing is written anywhere, nothing is stored server-side — the tree is rebuilt in your browser every time you load this page.
