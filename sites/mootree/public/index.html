🌳 mootree

This grows a family tree out of the people someone follows. Rows above the tree's owner are a generation each — how long ago that account was created, relative to the owner. Rows below are graph-verified lineage: a child has to actually follow the owner back, a grandchild has to follow that child back, and so on down the tree — an age gap alone doesn't earn a spot. Within a row, left-to-right is birth order — the actual order they followed people in, read straight off their repo, oldest follow first. Every read here is public atproto data — type any handle below, nothing to sign in to, ever.

source reads are all public atproto: your own app.bsky.graph.follow records (for follow order) and app.bsky.actor.getProfiles (for account-creation date). nothing is written anywhere, nothing is stored server-side — the tree is rebuilt in your browser every time you load this page.
