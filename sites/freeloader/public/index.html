freeloader

A cheat-sheet of AI/inference API free tiers, and a private tracker that reminds you to cancel before a trial quietly turns into a bill. It watches dates, not your wallet — you still sign up for things yourself and cancel them yourself.

known free tiers

A starting point, not a guarantee — hand-compiled, not live-checked against provider pages. Terms shift constantly and without notice: rate limits change, credit amounts change, "free" becomes "free with a card on file" becomes "gone." Verify on the provider's own pricing page before you rely on any of this.
