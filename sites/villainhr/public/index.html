    how the shortlist is actually built

    each SimCluster member's bio and up to 100 recent posts get scanned for villain-coded vocabulary (a real word list, weighted 40%), shouting ALL-CAPS words that aren't common acronyms (15%), chaos signal — exclamation density and villain-adjacent emoji (20%), lair-hours posting between midnight and 5am UTC (10%), and instigation — reply ratio and average post length as a monologue proxy (15%). the weighted sum is a 0-100 score; the strongest single axis picks an archetype. the cover letter and worktrial checklist quote real numbers and, where one exists, a real matching post as Exhibit A. the interview round is three actual reskinned technical-interview problems, graded against real test cases in your browser. it's real math and a real (tiny) coding test. the hiring committee is not real.
