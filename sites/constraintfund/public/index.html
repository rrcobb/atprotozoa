constraint fund / who should get funded

A board to nominate who should get funded to explore the space of generative constraints — the interesting kind of limits that make things worth making. Name someone, add a message on why if you like, and it goes on the board. Someone already nominated who you were thinking of? Hit ▲ vote instead — same person nominated twice just adds a vote, and near-identical entries (typos, spacing, punctuation) get merged automatically so votes don't split. This board is saved only in this browser; there is no shared server.

"many people should be exploring the space of generative constraints that make things interesting, and there should be funding for that, tbh" — @ver.ooo, the post this board exists to answer.
