About the Author

WENDELL OSRIC CRANE (they decline to specify further, citing “binary tyranny”) is the author of eleven previous volumes, four of which exist. Crane holds an MFA from a low-residency program they will not name “out of respect for the program.” Their honors include a Pushcart nomination they submitted themselves, a residency at a friend’s lake house they are still not sure counted, and the 2024 Silver Trowel for Excellence in Digging Deeper, awarded by an organization Crane also founded. Their work has appeared in several journals, most of which are the same journal, renamed. They live, as they insist on every jacket copy, “between the sacred and the self-checkout.”

End of Scanning

There is no lane. No scale, no scanner, no woman in Ohio.
There was a woman two towns from here who let children keep the receipt,
because to her it meant something, and so it did.
This book is that receipt. Thank you for shopping. Have a nice day.
I am trying. I mean it more than the machine ever could —
which, I’ve checked, is a very low bar, and I cleared it anyway.
