how scoring works
  • cadence — how evenly the vowels (I, E, A) are spaced through the consonants. bunched-up consonants tank it.
  • mouthfeel — how natural each adjacent letter pair is to say, judged by consonant/vowel class (nasal, plosive, fricative, liquid).
  • punch — how hard-hitting the consonants are, weighted toward the opening letter.
