🪮 you cannot comb a hairy ball flat

It's a real theorem. Any continuous field of hair lying flat against a sphere must have at least one cowlick — a point where a hair sticks straight up or swirls, because it can't decide which way to lie. It's topology, not bad grooming: it follows from the sphere having no "hairless" (zero) point a tangent vector field can avoid.

The torus gets to cheat — a donut can be combed perfectly flat, no cowlick required, since (unlike the sphere) it has a flat tangent field with nowhere to get stuck. Switch surfaces below and go looking for the singularity.

Drag across the fur to comb it — it remembers your stroke and relaxes back over a few seconds. Drag empty space to look around, WASD to fly, Q/E for up and down, shift to boost. Custom WebGL vertex shader bends every strand; nothing here is a texture.
