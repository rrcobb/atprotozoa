How it works

Paste any page's URL and spoonternet fetches it server-side, then swaps every consecutive pair of "content" words' leading sound — "the ham sandwich" becomes "the sam handwich," same swap pikiwedia runs on real Wikipedia articles. Links on the proxied page route back through spoonternet, so you can keep clicking around a whole site with everything spoonerized. Scripts and inline event handlers are stripped (and blocked by a strict CSP either way) — this is a reading toy, not a browser.
