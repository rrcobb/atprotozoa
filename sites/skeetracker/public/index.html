skeetracker

a step-sequencer tracker with custom Web Audio synths. export a song as a minified, gzipped skeet thread; load one back from a link. keyboard: z x c v b n m + s d g h j = notes, q w e r t y u row = an octave up, [ ] octave, 1 note-off, . clear, arrows navigate, space play/stop.

export → skeet thread

packs the song (json → gzip → base64) and splits it into skeet-sized chunks tagged so a thread of them can be reassembled. post part 1, then reply to your own post with part 2, reply to that with part 3, and so on — that reply chain is the song file. anyone can then paste a link to any post in that thread below to load it.
