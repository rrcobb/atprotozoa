case file · mutual-follow division

kevinmoot

Pick two accounts. We trace the shortest chain of moots (mutual follows) between them — six degrees of Kevin Bacon, except the co-star credit is a follow-back.

share on bluesky

a "moot" is a mutual follow — you follow them, they follow you. this crawls the public Bluesky AppView live, in your browser, to find the shortest chain of moots between two accounts; nothing is stored server-side, and the search gives up after a bounded amount of crawling rather than hammering the API forever.
