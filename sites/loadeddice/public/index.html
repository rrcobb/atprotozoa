loadeddice

Five lumpy polyhedra: two 7-sided, one 12-sided, two 20-sided, every face a different size. Throw them in the tray and the numbers on their top faces add up to a sum; the sum picks a cell on the grid (walking a Hilbert curve). Then let them evolve: face sizes and numbers mutate until the odds of each sum match the ink in the target picture.

evolving the dice…
A throw is real physics: the dice tumble in the tray and the face pointing up when they stop counts. "From the odds" skips the physics and samples each die's estimated landing odds, so thousands of rolls take a second.

Each die is a set of planes; moving a plane in grows its face, pushing it out shrinks it until the face vanishes. Fitness is how much the exact distribution of sums overlaps the target. Every generation: mutate planes, tilt normals, renumber faces, keep the fittest.
Each strip is one die: width = chance that face ends up on top, number = its value. Red slivers never come up.
