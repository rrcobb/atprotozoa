A regular mute list only catches the exact word. This one mutes the concept: mute "oysters" and it also catches "bivalve," "shellfish," "raw bar" — every enumerable near-miss a hand-written word-list would've had to spell out one by one. It's a tiny personal AppView: connects straight to the live Bluesky firehose in your browser, expands each concept you name into a cloud of related words via the free Datamuse API, and scores every incoming post against that cloud. Nothing is sent anywhere or stored on a server — your mute list lives only in this browser's local storage. Every mute shows its work: which concept, which word actually matched, so it's a rigorous framework whether or not you thought to type "maine" and "oyster."
