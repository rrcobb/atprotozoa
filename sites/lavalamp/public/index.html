🌋 lava lamp

Enter a handle and a dozen or so of their posts drift inside the lamp — the more likes a post got, the higher it floats; the fewer, the lower it sinks. A random mix of bangers and total flops, always. The first few words of each poke out past its blob. They cycle out for fresh ones every few seconds.

try: catblanketflower · bsky.app
🦋 share this lamp

Reads only public posts (Bluesky's anonymous AppView, getAuthorFeed, up to a few hundred recent originals). Height is likes on a log scale relative to that account's own pool — replies and reposts by the account are skipped. Nothing is posted or changed, it just reads and floats. Hover a blob for its like count, click to open the post.
