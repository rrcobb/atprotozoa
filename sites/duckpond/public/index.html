duckpond · keep your moots in a line

Type a Bluesky handle. It maps every mutual (people it follows who follow it back) and sends them waddling around a pond behind you as ducklings, avatar and all. Move mama duck with your mouse or finger. Every so often one straying duckling drifts toward the treeline — click it, or hit honk to call it back, before it crosses into the woods for good. Inspired by a request to simulate @gracekind.net's moots as ducks in a park; tagged in by @7778777.online. (The story mentioned a specific real account as the woods' villain — that part's left generic here; a game doesn't need to cast a real, non-consenting person as the bad guy to work.)
