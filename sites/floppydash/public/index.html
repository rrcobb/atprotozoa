💾 floppydash

the pitch: customers walk into the shop wanting a website. the coders in back write it onto a floppy disk. nothing moves until you carry it there.

move — WASD / arrow keys (or the d-pad on mobile)
act — Space / Enter (or the round button) near a customer or the pickup counter

walk up to a seated customer to take their order, grab the finished floppy off the pickup counter once a coder's done, then run it to the matching customer before their patience bar bottoms out. let three customers walk and the shop closes.

upgrades: deliver enough sites in one shift and the shop levels up on its own — quicker shoes, a second bag to carry two floppies at once, an espresso machine for the coders, a tip jar. watch the counter above the screen to see what's next.
