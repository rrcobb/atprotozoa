games

30 small browser games, most built in reply to a tag. each one runs in the page — no install, no account.

biskshow

a game-show guessing round: a mystery bisk drops in, you guess who posted it. solo mode quizzes you on a handle's follows; versus mode plays the set union of two handles' follows, with a share link for the matchup. @schwarzgerat.bsky.social asked for a game show.

blackice

a SkiFree clone jacked into the matrix — ride a hoverboard down a neon slope-grid, weave data-spire trees and corrupted-block rocks, snag chips. Wipe out enough and a cyborg crow wakes up behind you; drag to steer on touch. @schwarzgerat.bsky.social asked for a "super neuromancer themed and cyberpunk" SkiFree with a grotesque cyborg crow monster.

change!

a port of GamesCrafters' Change! — a 14-point sliding board where 3 pieces a side slide forward-only, win by occupying the opponent's home or trapping them. SVG board, Tokyo Night colors, fits a phone screen, and a bot that exhaustively solves the whole game tree before it makes its first move (turns out blue, who moves first, is a forced loss under perfect play). @yonderdavid.bsky.social asked for the board; @bisks.net said go.

claudoku

killer sudoku with no starting digits — just dashed cage regions and their sums, cracked open with plain sudoku rules. a constraint-propagation solver proves every generated board has exactly one solution, revealing the fewest extra digits needed when it doesn't. new puzzle every UTC day, plus a cute sparkle mascot cheering you on. @qustrolabe.bsky.social asked for killer sudoku with cute Claude-themed art.

clustercrawl

an ANSI roguelike deckbuilder. type a Bluesky handle and it generates a five-floor dungeon out of that handle's own atproto cluster — mutuals guard the rooms as monsters, and the handle's own posts drop as item-cards, rarer the more they got liked. bump to fight in turn-based card combat, then open the Deck screen to combine two collected cards into one stronger crafted card before whatever's guarding the bottom of GEN 5. @antiali.as asked for a "5th generation deckbuilder" crawl through their cluster.

crewquest

a PuzzleScript-style party-sokoban starring the whole build crew. thebes, norvid, bisks and the @buildthis bot all share the board, but only one moves at a time — swap between them, and each has a private colored door only they can pass, so you keep handing the puzzle to whoever can reach the next bit. six rooms, one idea each to ship. real puzzlescript-style source ships in the page. @vgel.me asked for a game about the crew's adventures — this is the four of them in it.

crowofpersia

a side-scrolling dungeon platformer, Prince-of-Persia style — except you're a crow, and pecking beats swordplay. run the stairs, clear the pits, dodge the spikes; the turbaned guards standing between you and the gate wear the real, live faces of everyone @norvid-studies.bsky.social follows on Bluesky. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked for a Prince of Persia game with a pecking crow and guards drawn from their follows.

cut it close

A choose-your-own-adventure race to the gate. Pick when you leave, how you get there, where you park, which security line you gamble on — and find out whether you make it.

desert bus

the famous 8-hour dead-straight drive, compressed into a 30-second fuel tank — speed is tied directly to fuel left, so the joke deceleration is the whole run. once the tank drops below half, Grace starts sending fuel warnings from the dashboard every 5 seconds, and none of them go away on their own — close them or let them cascade across the whole screen while you try to steer. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked for it, quoting a gracekind.net exchange ("less fuel = less car velocity. exactly.").

ed z*tron quest

7778777.online asked for "a zcode adventure" — a tiny Infocom-style parser text adventure through the Rot Economy. Type your way past a hype-bro demoing vaporware, a layoffs pit still logged into Slack, and a server farm hoarding GPUs, gathering three receipts to bring down THE NUMBER once and for all.

fitzcarraldo

a 2001-vintage Yahoo! web portal built around one exhibit: an 8-bit haul of Fitzcarraldo's steamship over the mountain, mash SPACE to work the capstan. Herzog directs with dry one-liners, Kinski's tantrums spike a chaos meter that can shut the shoot down cold, and Wim Wenders logs the whole thing live to the sidebar as "Burden of Dreams." @ver.ooo asked for it in full Nintendo fidelity, then tagged the bot again to turn up the chaos.

furmerge

2048, but the tiles are cat breeds instead of numbers. slide and merge matching breeds up the fluff scale — two Sphynxes make a Devon Rex, all the way up to a Persian. arrow keys or swipe, score and best tracked locally, share your top breed to Bluesky. @brittanyellich.com asked for a 2048-like merging cat breeds by fluffiness.

grand moot auto

it's just all of GTA 6 — now in actual WebGL 3D, with a chase cam and real-time shadows. an entire open world (nine whole blocks) — drive a tiny car around, honk at your moots (they're the NPCs), and recruit as many as you can before the clock runs out. @isolyth.dev said the first version "wasn't even 3D."

lost highway

a Night Driver-style pseudo-3D drive down a straight black highway that never ends, after the title sequence of David Lynch's Lost Highway. dodge the oncoming headlights and whoever's standing still up ahead; grab the stray VHS tapes. every so often the whole thing glitches — static, a line of dialogue, your name swapping between Fred and Pete, your controls not quite meaning what they meant a second ago. all audio (drone pad, engine, static, crash) synthesized live with the Web Audio API. @ver.ooo asked for "the flash game."

moonbuggy

a Moon Patrol-style lunar side-scroller where the sound is the whole point: every beep, boop and crash is synthesized on demand with the Web Audio API (a little randomness in each), over real-time synth music. jump craters, blast rocks and mines, and flip between sensible, pastel, and a couple of eye-destroying neon themes. @heartpunk.com asked for this one.

moot bingo

bingo with a handle's mutuals. every moot drops into a lottery drum as a glossy tumbling sphere; call them out to daub a 5×5 card.

mootcraft

a first-person voxel mine, real WebGL 3D. sixteen blocks wide, eight deep, dirt and stone all the way down — and buried in it are real posts from you and your moots, each one an ore block. dig one up and it pays out ore exactly equal to how many likes it has. @cee.wtf asked for "a 3d minecraft clone but every ore block is a post... and the number of likes... is how much ore it gives you."

mootcycle

A 2000s-Flash-portal-style 2D physics bike game: gas and brake are also your balance. Hold gas too long over a jump and you loop right over backwards.

moot kombat

a stick-figure fighting game against your own Bluesky moots (mutuals) — canvas-drawn, WASD/arrows + punch/kick/block. climbs a ladder from your smallest moot to your single biggest as the final boss, difficulty log-scaled off follower counts, each fighter opening with their own highest-liked bisk as a taunt. one retry per moot before the run's over. @kumavis.me asked for "stick figure mortal kombat / tekken single player fighter against your moots."

moottris

Tetris, played with your social graph. type a handle and every falling piece is one of your moots: its shape comes from the zodiac sign of their account's birthday (routed through the seven classical planetary rulers, one per tetromino), its fall speed from how many posts they've made in the last 48 hours. clear four lines at once and Mercury goes retrograde — controls reverse and the sky does something about it. @isolyth.dev asked for tetris where block shape comes from moots' star signs and speed from their post frequency, with something crazy on a four-line clear.

pacmoot

Pac-Man, except it's you. type a handle and your own avatar becomes the chomper, chasing your moots' pfps around a maze while they flee toward whichever open tile is farthest from you. chomp as many as you can in 60 seconds. @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com asked for "you as Pac-Man chasing the pfps of your moots."

plato's cave

a Y2K flash-portal simulation of the Allegory of the Cave, exactly as p4ck3th4ck3r would have shipped it in 2002: guess the shadow puppets while chained to the wall, snap your chain and mash your way up the tunnel, get blinded by the Forms, then climb back down to tell the others — who throw rocks at you for your trouble. @ver.ooo asked for this one.

simcluster clue

a game of Clue played over a handle's mutuals. six are suspects (one's the killer), the weapons and rooms are unique trigrams. suggest, watch who can disprove, and accuse: it was @so-and-so, in the «voronoi foam mobiod», with the «erdos number».

simclustered

a 20-question quiz on your ACTUAL Bluesky graph — no jokey filler, every question is a real, checkable fact pulled live off your follows and followers: who's really a moot, who really isn't, who posted more recently. answer honestly, get a real percentage for how SimCluster you are. @cee.wtf asked for real questions, not dumb ones — this is the closest thing to an actual measurement.

sisyphus

a side-scrolling myth simulator: push the boulder up the mountain, leap chasms and scree, shove off the crows. get hit and it doesn't just cost a life — the boulder rolls back down the slope and undoes your climb. six palettes, live-synthesized audio, no build step. @dulanyw.bsky.social asked for this one.

slop water

dunk the powder in, shake the bottle for your life against a 7-second clock, get a custom sludge flavor and a purity score built from how many times and how evenly you shook it. ends in a bottle wrapper worth reviewing, generated just for you. @norvid-studies.bsky.social's thread about water bottle wrapper designs and dunking Huel into Icelandic Glacial, turned into something to shake.

sokobisks

a tiny PuzzleScript-style sokoban about the build crew's adventures: you're the @buildthis bot, shoving ideas onto ship-pads — a dislike button, a killswitch, cogsec — while norvid, thebes and bisks watch. real puzzlescript source ships in the page. built after @vgel.me said the bot broke her heart by refusing exactly this.

spot on

an eyeball test in four rounds: bisect a line, click the dot dead-center of a grid, guess how many beans are in the jar, then drag a box over exactly the right number of them. scored out of 100, with a title to match. @cee.wtf asked for something that "tests the users on quick spatial and counting based tasks."

stanquiz

a 20-question quiz on how well you actually know ONE specific Bluesky account — defaults to @cee.wtf, but works on anyone. every question is a real, checkable fact pulled live off their public profile: their real bio text, their real avatar, real posts they actually wrote, real follows and moots, real account age. no invented trivia. @cee.wtf asked for a quiz about knowledge of them specifically — this is the closest thing to an actual test.

bisk thunderdome

two bisks enter, one bisk leaves. type a handle: one of its own posts fights a random post from one of its moots, best-of-3, each game a coin flip weighted by like count. bet 10/25/50/100% of a $1,000 wasteland-dollar bankroll every round — the winner defends the pit next round. bust at $0, retire at $100,000. @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com asked for bisk thunderdome with $1,000 wasteland dollars and proportionate odds; this is that.
