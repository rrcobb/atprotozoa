Inspired by @fromthewestmeadow.com's PCA-on-embeddings visualization of their own posts and their mutuals' — this is an independent, live implementation of the same underlying idea, minus the 3D picture: a small server watches the open Bluesky firehose, computes a real sentence embedding for a filtered trickle of posts, and ranks them by cosine similarity against the centroid of that account's last 100 posts (also real embeddings, not a bag-of-words trick). No login, nothing stored beyond the ranked list below — every post here was already public on the open firehose.

How the scoring actually works: the target's last ~100 posts (via the public AppView, no login) are each embedded with Cloudflare Workers AI (bge-base-en-v1.5) and averaged into one centroid vector, refreshed every 20 minutes. The live firehose is filtered down — English, long enough to carry meaning, not obvious spam, one candidate per author per 45 seconds — because the full firehose is far more volume than any embeddings budget survives; that filtered trickle is embedded in small batches on a timer and scored against the centroid. The leaderboard keeps the highest-scoring posts seen in roughly the last 6 hours, so it drifts as the firehose does — this is a sample of the firehose, not exhaustive coverage of it.
