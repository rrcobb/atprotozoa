revolver · live russian roulette, played in dares

Load a dare, challenge another Bluesky handle, and once you both agree, take turns pulling the trigger — live. One bullet, chambers of your choosing. Whoever's still standing at the end gets to see the loser's dare land on their timeline.

How it actually posts: it really does, automatically. Lose, and the winner's dare goes out from your account the instant the chamber comes up loaded — no confirmation, no undo. That's why you log in with Bluesky before you can play, and why you'll see a plain explanation of exactly that, with a box to check, before login ever starts. You'll see both dares before you agree to a round, and you can back out any time before the trigger's pulled.

login required to play (Bluesky OAuth, scoped to posting — one write permission, nothing else) so a loss can really post; spectating a round needs no login. reads Bluesky's public profile API to show avatars and handles. sibling of bisks.net.
