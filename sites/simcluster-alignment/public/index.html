how the reading actually works

your recent posts and every SimCluster member's recent posts (mutuals, widened to follows if you don't have enough) each become a word-frequency vector, a hashtag set, an average post length / reply ratio / emoji rate, and a 24-bucket UTC hour-of-day histogram. the cluster's vectors get summed, not averaged one-to-one, into a single communal-mind signal, and your vector is scored against that sum with cosine similarity on vocabulary and rhythm, Jaccard overlap on hashtags, and a normalized distance on posting style — a weighted average of whichever axes have data, 0–100. the dominant axis picks an element (Air vocabulary, Fire hashtags, Water style, Earth rhythm) and a chakra, because a percentage alone doesn't feel very spiritual. it's real math. the spirituality part we made up.
