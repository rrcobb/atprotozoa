ideahose / a crowdsourced backlog for @buildthis

Watches the whole live firehose — not just posts tagging the bot — for anything that reads like a website/app/tool idea, groups the ones different people separately keep having, and ranks the backlog by how many people wanted it, how those posts actually did, and upvotes from right here.     Sampled from the live firehose via Jetstream — root-level posts only, matched against a keyword/phrase heuristic ("someone should build…", "idea for a website…", "@buildthis…" and similar), a fraction of the total stream, not every idea posted. Matching ideas are grouped by shared significant keywords — cheap overlap clustering, not real language understanding, so it's an honest approximation: it can lump two different ideas that happen to share a distinctive word, and it can miss two phrasings of the same idea that share no words. Score = mentions × 30 + log2(reactions) × 8 + upvotes × 12, with a small bump for ideas already posted straight at the bot. Upvotes are capped at one per browser/idea per hour. asked for by @words.bsky.social · built by @buildthis.bisks.net
