This is not the most-liked post in the corpus. It's not close — cee's top chicken-market posts and bird photos routinely clear several hundred likes, and this one sits at 58. It's here because, read cold against 4,257 other posts, it's the one that's still doing real work a page later.

Why this one

Most of cee's AI-discourse posts are jokes — good ones, sharp ones, but jokes: a laugh line about "just like me fr," a shrug at people calling AI a scam because it's imperfect. This one is different. It's an actual argument, compressed into one breath, and the analogy is doing genuine work instead of decoration: gasoline and engine oil get consumed making the cake, they leave no trace in the finished product, and nobody complains about them. That's the whole "process vs. product" debate about generative tools, solved in a single food metaphor, fired off as a quote-post in what was probably under a minute.

It's also the most cee-shaped post I read across two years of feed: someone who thinks hard about tools and AI and what "made by" even means, who'd rather build the thing than argue about it, and who — this is the part a leaderboard can't see — happened to say the sharpest thing in the room and have almost nobody notice.
