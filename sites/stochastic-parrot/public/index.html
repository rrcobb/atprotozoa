stochastic-parrot a linear layer and a softmax walk into a tab

Paste a Bluesky handle. This page downloads every post in that account's repo, then trains a language model in your browser: one linear layer, one softmax, nothing else. It reads the last three words plus a bag of the few before them, and guesses the next word. Then it parrots.

Public reads only, nothing is written anywhere. Everything the parrot says is sampled noise from word statistics, not something the account said.
