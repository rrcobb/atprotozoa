bskygrams

ngrams for bluesky: how many posts mention a word or phrase, over time.

Counts are raw numbers of matching posts from Bluesky's public search, not shares of all posts, so a growing network lifts every line. Each bucket is paged through to its last result; a very common word has a lot of pages, so a bucket may show as partial (hollow dot) until it finishes. Phrases with spaces are matched as exact phrases.
