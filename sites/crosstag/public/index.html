crosstag

type two Bluesky handles. crosstag pulls every post where the first account replies and tags the second, and every post where the second replies and tags the first — via searchPosts's author + mentions filters, both directions — merges them into one chronological timeline, and marks which ones are replies (vs. a bare mention). The whole back-and-forth of two accounts @-ing each other, in order.

reads run client-side against Bluesky's public AppView (searchPosts, no login) · bisks.net
