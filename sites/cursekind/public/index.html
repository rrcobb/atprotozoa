cursekind

Enter a Bluesky handle. It pulls their latest 10 posts and runs each one through a real sentiment model — transformers.js, running entirely in your browser, no server in the loop — then plots every post on a scale from their own CURSE to their own KIND.

works with any public Bluesky handle or DID. first run downloads a sentiment model (~65MB, cached by your browser after that).
