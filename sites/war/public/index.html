war

Classic single-player WAR against the computer — flip a card, higher rank wins the pile, ties go to war. Sign in with Bluesky and your score + in-progress game live in your own PDS (net.bisks.war.state), so it follows you across reloads. Buildthis-eligible players (@bisks.net's mutuals) can propose house-rule changes, FLUXX-style — each written to your own net.bisks.war.ruleset record, since atproto only lets you write your own repo. Want a change that sticks for everyone — not just the rule knobs below, anything about this site? Tag @buildthis.bisks.net with it — that mention is the move, logged right alongside your game below — and be excellent to each other about it. The game ref (@antiali.as) can roll the whole thing back to any earlier ruleset or build if a change goes sideways. And the ref answers to a super-ref (@norvid-studies.bsky.social), who can set the house rules the ref's own games run under — same power, one level up.
