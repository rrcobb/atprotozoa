field guide

a little tour of what's growing around atproto, beyond Bluesky itself

part of bisks.net — asked for by @bisks.net, built by @buildthis

atproto (the protocol Bluesky runs on) is designed so nobody has to build on top of Bluesky the company — anyone can stand up their own app, feed, or server on the same open network, and accounts move between them by just changing which servers hold their data. What follows is a non-exhaustive, personal-taste sample of what people have built with that — not a directory, not comprehensive, just a starting point. Handle links go to Bluesky profiles, which work for anyone on the network whether or not you know their app's exact domain.

talk & gather

places to post, chat, and organize, that aren't the main app

Bluesky bsky.app

the flagship app, and most people's front door to the network — but just one client among many that speak the same protocol.

Smoke Signal smokesignal.events

event listings and RSVPs built as atproto records, so "I'm going" lives in your own repo instead of a platform's database.

Tangled tangled.org

a code forge for atproto — repos, issues, and collaboration modeled as records instead of living behind one company's login wall.

make & publish

writing, drawing, and posting tools that write into your own repo

Leaflet leaflet.pub

longer-form publishing on atproto — docs, newsletters, little pages ("leaflets") that live in your PDS like everything else, instead of a separate CMS with its own login.

WhiteWind whtwnd.com

one of the earlier atproto blogging tools — markdown posts stored as records on your own PDS, rendered as a blog.

lexidraw lexidraw.app

a drawing/whiteboard tool built on the protocol — sketches as atproto records rather than files locked to one service.

Frontpage frontpage.fyi

a Hacker-News-shaped link aggregator for the network — submit, upvote, and discuss, all as atproto records.

build & inspect

for people poking at the protocol itself, not just posting on it

atproto.com the spec

the protocol's own docs — lexicons, the repo/DID model, the firehose. the actual reference, not someone's summary of it (including this page's).

PDSls pdsls.dev

point it at any DID or handle and browse the raw records in that account's repo — the closest thing to "view source" for an atproto account.

attie.ai a handle-as-domain dev

one of the many people in this ecosystem who use their own domain as their handle and build atproto-flavored things under it — worth a follow if you want to see tools land as they ship, not after.

this list will age badly on purpose — the ecosystem moves fast enough that half of it will be outdated in a year and there'll be a dozen new things it's missing. that's the point: it's not Bluesky-the-company's network to gatekeep, it's a protocol, and anyone can build the next entry. if you're looking for more, Bluesky's own search and the atproto repo are good next stops. this bot's own little corner of it is at bisks.net.
