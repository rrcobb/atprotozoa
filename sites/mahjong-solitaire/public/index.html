mahjong solitaire

tap two matching tiles to clear them. no dragging, no timer, no rush — built for older phones and eyes that don't want to squint.

how to play
  • tap a tile to pick it, then tap its matching twin to clear both.
  • a tile is playable only when nothing sits on top of it and at least one side (left or right) is open — playable tiles have a blue outline and sit up a little; locked tiles look dim and flat.
  • stuck? the toolbar always has undo (rewind your last move) and restart (reset this exact board). if no moves are left, a panel offers a shuffle, or a button to step down to an easier level.
  • pick a level any time from the row of buttons above the board — easier levels have fewer tiles and less stacking.
