softscroll the sweet sweet software

Your moots post a lot. Some of it is software somebody made — a game, a tool, a repo, a weird little site — and the rest is weather. This reads you and your mutuals, sorts every post from the last few days into software or not, and scrolls you only the software, one day at a time.

Public reads only, no login. The sorting runs in your browser on rules (links to repos and app hosts, "I built…" voice, release language) so it will miss some and catch a few impostors — flip strictness if the feed is too thin or too wet.
