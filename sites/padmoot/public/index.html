padmoot

A social synthesizer / beat pad for atproto. Same pattern underneath, three controller skins on top: an MPC finger-drumming grid, a Launchpad session grid, and a TR-909 step strip — switch any time, nothing you've made is lost. Save your beat as a net.bisks.padmoot.pattern record in your own PDS, export it to disk as JSON or a real WAV, or jam on a pattern one of your moots made and save your own remix.
