commonplacea tumblr-esque standard.site editor

quick posts, straight to your PDS.

commonplace is a small tumblr-esque composer for standard.site — the shared publishing lexicons that Leaflet, pckt.blog and Offprint all speak. Write something, pick which of your publications it belongs to, and it's written as a site.standard.document record on your own repo. No new account — just sign in with your existing atproto handle.

titles default to sha256(did + unix-millis) at publish time, per the site.standard.document spec requiring a title. picking one or more publications above creates one document record per publication, all sharing this text.
