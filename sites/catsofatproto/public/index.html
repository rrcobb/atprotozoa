cats of atproto has been retired

This page used to show cat photos live off the Bluesky firehose. Because it displayed a stream of unvetted, third-party images pulled straight from the firehose as they were posted, Google's Safe Browsing crawler flagged it as a "deceptive page" — and that flag applied to the whole bisks.net domain, putting a warning in front of every site here.

Rather than try to filter a live public image stream, the experiment was taken down. Nothing here was ever deceptive or collected any personal information; the trigger was simply serving a firehose of images nobody had reviewed.

It was originally built at the request of @norvid-studies.bsky.social — the original thread is here.
