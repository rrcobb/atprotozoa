how does this work?

Each handle's public profile (app.bsky.actor.getProfile) feeds four stats: Sightings from post count, Believers from raw follower count, Skepticism from follower/follow ratio, Lore from bio length and profile polish. Whichever stat is highest picks a cryptid pool — high Sightings summons a Mothman, Thunderbird, or Flatwoods Monster; high Believers a Sasquatch, Nessie, or Champ; high Skepticism a Skinwalker, Dogman, or Fresno Nightcrawler; high Lore a Jersey Devil, Chupacabra, or Wendigo — and a seeded roll on the account's own DID picks the exact cryptid from that pool, so a dossier looks the same no matter who it's matched against. A clash score combines each cryptid's stats with a couple more seeded rolls to decide the winner and how the writeup reads. The whole thing is seeded off a hash of both DIDs together, so the same two handles always get the same front page.
