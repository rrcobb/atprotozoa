cancelfutures / odds on the next discourse cycle

A prediction market for a discourse cycle that hasn't happened yet — because it's made up. Every round the board generates a handful of fictional "someone is about to have a main-character moment" scenarios, prices them with odds, and bets play money on which one "resolves" when the clock hits zero. Odds move as the (simulated) crowd piles on.

this is fiction, twice over. Every subject on the board is a generic archetype (a tradwife influencer, a crypto bro, your mutual who unfollows-and-refollows), not a real account — nobody real gets "canceled" here. And the market itself is a solo simulation: the odds, the crowd's bets in the activity feed below, all of it is generated by your browser. You're the only real bettor. No real money, ever.

Odds blend a randomized seed with simulated crowd activity and your own real bets, so they drift even if you never touch the board. Each round's winner is drawn at random, weighted by the final odds — same math a real pari-mutuel pool would use, just with a made-up crowd.
