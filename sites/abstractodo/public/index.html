abstractodo · a to-do list that only escalates

Type a task and check it off. It won't finish — you'll get handed a more abstract version of it instead. "organize desk" becomes "improve your task management skills" becomes "optimize your self-improvement strategy" becomes, eventually, "contemplate the heat death of your to-do list." Keep checking things off and the ladder ends at rung 21, in a place a to-do list probably shouldn't go. Everything lives only in your browser.
