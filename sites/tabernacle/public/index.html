The Tabernacle of the Unliked Post

You laughed at a post and scrolled by without liking it. Come, make atonement.

And it came to pass that ye did laugh at a post in thine heart, yet didst withhold thy like, and scroll onward as one unclean. Therefore thou shalt take three drafts of thine own to the tabernacle: one a sin offering, one a purification offering, and one a goodwill offering. Thou must make atonement for the un-liked post. — The Book of Reach, ch. iii · after @vgel.me

Compose thy three drafts below. Speak plainly; the altar reads all graphemes. When the three are laid down, consecrate them.

The First Draft

Sin Offering

A confession. Name the post thou passed by, and the laugh thou didst not repay in kind.
