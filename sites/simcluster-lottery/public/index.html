simcluster lottery

Buy tickets, watch three clocks run down, and see who takes the pot. Every ticket is one entry; the draw picks a single winner weighted by tickets held, and they take the whole pot. It's play money only — coins can't be bought or cashed out for anything real, and every wallet resets to a fresh 1000 on the 1st of the month, so the leaderboard is really a monthly race, not a lifetime one.
