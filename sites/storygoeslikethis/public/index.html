THE STORY GOES LIKE THIS.

a theatrical re-enactment, cast and performed live in your browser

Fourteen posts from norvidstudies.substack.com's anthology of internet accounts each riffing on the same refrain — "the story goes like this:" — staged here as a little play. Every cast member speaks in a distinct synthesized voice (pitch and rate derived from their own handle, so the assignment is stable but not hand-picked). No ffmpeg ran anywhere and no Workers AI neuron was spent — this is the browser's own speechSynthesis, staged and captioned. Asked for by @words.bsky.social, replying to @norvid-studies.bsky.social's post of the link.
