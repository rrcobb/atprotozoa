why does this feel so good

every touch gives points, so there's no wrong move — that's positive reinforcement with a 100% payout rate. touching fast builds a combo multiplier, so the reward compounds the longer you keep going. the bonus stoves pay out on a random timer instead of a fixed one, which is the same variable-ratio schedule that makes slot machines and notification badges hard to put down — you can't predict the payout, so you keep checking. none of it is disguised. it's just laid out plainly, right down to the sound getting a little more excited every time you're on a streak.
