peemyplants.

a map of gardens that could use some free, nitrogen-rich fertilizer, for people who happen to be carrying some. list a spot, find a spot, water something.

this is a bit, not a directory. the pins already on the map are made up — no real addresses, no real gardens. if you "list a garden," the pin only ever lives in your own browser's local storage — it is never sent anywhere, never public, never seen by anyone else. also: public urination is illegal basically everywhere. this site doesn't invite you to do it, it's a joke about the idea of it. touch grass, not literally.
