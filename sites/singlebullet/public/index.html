The case for a second gun, and what happened to it

The loudest argument for a shooter on the grassy knoll was never geometric — it was acoustic. A 1978 House Select Committee analysis of a Dallas police Dictabelt recording claimed to find a fourth shot, timed to the knoll. In 1982 a National Academy of Sciences panel reviewed the same recording and found the gunshot-like impulses it relied on occurred about a minute after the shooting, cross-talk from a channel recorded elsewhere — not gunfire at Dealey Plaza at all. No physical, ballistic, or (per that review) acoustic evidence has held up placing a second shooter on the knoll.
