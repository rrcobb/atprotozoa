leave the Void

Deletes every net.bisks.void.* record you own from your own PDS, in batches — shouts, echoes, murmurs and votes first, your participation record last — then purges this browser's local derived cache of your activity. This does not touch any other record in your repo, and it cannot be undone from here (your data is really gone from your PDS, not just hidden).
