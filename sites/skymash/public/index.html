about

skymash is a head-to-head comparison game for Bluesky profiles, Facemash-style: two accounts, their latest posts, click the one you like more. Built off a spec posted by a mutual of Rob's, linking to a static write-up at gideon-goddard.github.io.

cluster score

Nomination is open to everyone — any Bluesky account can be nominated into the comparison pool, no threshold to clear. (Originally there was a 40+ cluster-score bar; @fromthewestmeadow.com asked to open it up to everyone on the site, not just the simcluster people, so it's gone.) Each nomination still records a cluster score for context: the account's mutual-follow pool size — everyone who follows them and is followed back — widened to plain follows if that pool is too thin to be meaningful. This is the same "community positioning" metric the sites/simcluster* family already uses across this constellation. It is not a follower count, and it's shown purely as flavor now, not a gate.

ranking

Rankings come purely from head-to-head votes, replayed as standard Elo (starting rating 1500, K=32) in the order votes were cast. Nothing else — no likes, no follower counts, no engagement — feeds into the number.

matchmaking

Most matchups pair an account with a similarly-rated neighbor, so close contests stay close instead of a 1500 facing a 2000. About 30% of the time the second pick is fully random instead — that's what surfaces brand-new nominees (no votes yet, so no reliable rating) and keeps the leaderboard from calcifying around the same few accounts always facing each other.

anti-manipulation & transparency

Every nomination and every vote is a public record on the nominator's or voter's own atproto repo — net.bisks.skymash.nomination and net.bisks.skymash.vote — not on a server this site controls. A vote's record key is the matchup pair, so one signed-in account can only ever have one live vote per pair; re-voting a pair overwrites the old vote rather than stacking duplicates. There is no way to vote without a Bluesky account, and no way to vote on a pair more than once per account. The whole leaderboard is reproducible by anyone from the same public records skymash reads.

opting out

Nomination doesn't require the nominated account's consent — that's intentional, it's the whole "earned, not opted-into" premise — but anyone can remove themselves from the pool by signing in and writing a net.bisks.skymash.optout record to their own repo (see nominate). skymash only honors an opt-out written by the account it names, so no one else can opt you in or out on your behalf. Once written, it excludes that account from the pool regardless of who nominates it, past or future.
