about

skymash is a head-to-head comparison game for Bluesky profiles, Facemash-style: two eligible accounts, their latest posts, click the one you like more. Built off a spec posted by a mutual of Rob's, linking to a static write-up at gideon-goddard.github.io.

eligibility

Only accounts with a cluster score of 40 or higher can be nominated into the comparison pool. The score is the account's mutual-follow pool size — everyone who follows them and is followed back — widened to plain follows if that pool is too thin to be meaningful. This is the same "community positioning" metric the sites/simcluster* family already uses across this constellation. It is not a follower count: a large, mostly one-directional following does not clear the bar on its own.

ranking

Rankings come purely from head-to-head votes, replayed as standard Elo (starting rating 1500, K=32) in the order votes were cast. Nothing else — no likes, no follower counts, no engagement — feeds into the number.

anti-manipulation & transparency

Every nomination and every vote is a public record on the nominator's or voter's own atproto repo — net.bisks.skymash.nomination and net.bisks.skymash.vote — not on a server this site controls. A vote's record key is the matchup pair, so one signed-in account can only ever have one live vote per pair; re-voting a pair overwrites the old vote rather than stacking duplicates. There is no way to vote without a Bluesky account, and no way to vote on a pair more than once per account. The whole leaderboard is reproducible by anyone from the same public records skymash reads.
