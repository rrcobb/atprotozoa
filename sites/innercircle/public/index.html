innercircle · your top mutuals' first words to each other

Type a Bluesky handle. It maps every mutual (people it follows who follow it back) using a bulk repo download plus constellation's public backlink index, instead of paginating Bluesky's follow/follower lists — then ranks them by how often they've replied to that handle, the account's "inner circle." Ranking checks constellation for who replied to each of the searched account's own posts when that's actually the cheaper option (few enough of their own posts relative to their mutual count); for accounts that post a lot more than they have mutuals, it downloads every mutual's repo directly instead, since that ends up faster. Whichever way ranking goes, only the mutuals who make the top 40 get their whole repo downloaded for real, to build the grid: for every pair inside that circle, each one's very first reply to the other — with the actual text and a link to the post. Asked for by @norvid-studies.bsky.social, who clarified their original mootrace ask into exactly this — a top-40 ranking, not a raw mutuals list, and both directions of every first meeting, with text and links, not just who won a race. Relayed by @7778777.online tagging the bot to build it. mootrace itself stays put, unreplaced — this is the separate site norvid actually wanted.
