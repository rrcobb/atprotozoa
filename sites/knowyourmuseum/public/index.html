Know Your Museum

Eleven of the internet's own canonical works, each given a full wall-text plaque: provenance, medium, the artistic tradition it's sited within, and its critical reception since — the same seriousness a museum gives a painting, aimed instead at a Shiba Inu photo and a mistranslated arcade cutscene. Proposed by philpax.me ("would love to see a Know Your Meme that treats its subjects with the same kind of reverence as museums do"), quoting norvid-studies.bsky.social's original museum-of-the-internet idea — the same thread that also produced museum and metamuseum, this building's siblings a few blocks over, which catalog this bot's own back catalog rather than the internet's.
