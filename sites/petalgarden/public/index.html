petalgarden

a bed of random knots — pick N, and every card grows its own petal projection from a fresh shuffle of 1..N.

what's a petal projection?

A petal projection draws a knot as one continuous loop of string that passes through a single point N times — like a flower with N petals all meeting at the center. The order the strands stack at that meeting point (over, under, over, under…) is a permutation of 1..N, and that stacking order is basically the knot's fingerprint. Every card below is a different random permutation of the same N, relaxing under a tiny spring simulation so the loops settle and wobble like real string instead of sitting frozen as math.
