breadboard-555

two players · one 555 each · one part per round · first to oscillate wins

post it to bluesky
how to play
  1. Take turns. On your turn do one thing: pick a part from your stack and click two holes on your board to place it, or click a part already on your board to pick it up and click two new holes to move it (or "put it back" to cancel without spending your turn), or pass.
  2. Your board has the 555's eight pins plus a V+ rail (top) and a GND rail (bottom). Wires join holes into one net.
  3. The first circuit that makes the 555 oscillate wins. Wire up a real astable: supply and ground on the chip, reset high, trigger tied to threshold, a capacitor to ground, and a resistor route that charges it. Two ways work (one uses pin 7 and two resistors, one feeds back from pin 3 through a single resistor).
  4. Stack contents are the same for both players, and a diode and an inductor are in there to waste your turns. Short V+ to GND and you let the smoke out, so don't.
