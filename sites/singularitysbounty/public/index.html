The pitch

"language is a construction set for hacking the human mind" — the bio, in full sincerity, above three more lines about LLMs and a note that posts aren't financial advice

Roughly 40 posts a day for almost two years, and the throughline isn't chaos, it's one question asked from a dozen directions: what is a language model actually doing, and what does it mean that talking to one changed how you think about talking at all. Some days that question comes out as a lyric prompt. Some days it's a toy shipped to yuwakisa.com before lunch. Some days it's a genuinely sharp one-liner dropped into a reply and never followed up on. The pinned post is a fake propaganda poster of a man leading a crowd under the slogan "Not just part of the problem — Part of the meta-problem." That's the whole pitch, delivered as a bit: aware of the discourse, inside the discourse, still building anyway.
