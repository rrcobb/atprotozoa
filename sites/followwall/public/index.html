🦋 share to Bluesky

Reads only public data (Bluesky's anonymous AppView — getFollowers, getProfile). Everything runs in your browser. Capped at 4,000 followers (40 pages) so a huge account doesn't turn one page load into thousands of requests — the follower-count badge at top is the real total even when the wall below is a partial sample. Nothing is posted or changed — it just reads.
