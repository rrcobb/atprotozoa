mootdrone

type a Bluesky handle, then pick or randomize which moots (mutuals, widened to follows if there aren't enough) land on the board — up to eight. each becomes one synthesized voice: an LFO rate from their hashed DID, an instrument from their hashed bio (pad / horn / vox / bell / drumkit), and brightness from how often they've been posting lately. mute, solo, loop, mix, reselect the lineup any time, layer in an optional alpha/beta/delta/theta binaural base tone — then grab a clip of the network breathing and post it to Bluesky as a video.

optional base tone, layered under the network — headphones on: two plain sine tones, one per ear, a few Hz apart. your brain perceives the difference as a slow pulsing binaural beat at that rate, unrelated to any voice on the board.

built on atproto · public AppView reads only, mixed live in the browser · synthesis is plain Web Audio, no samples · moots-graph logic copied from clustercrawl · sharing signs you in with Bluesky OAuth (no password stored) to post a clip as a video, straight from your browser
