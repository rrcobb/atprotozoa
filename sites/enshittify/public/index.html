Grandma Cheryl's 3-Ingredient Banana Bread

Makes one loaf · 10 minutes prep · 55 minutes bake

You need:

  • 3 very ripe (spotty/black) bananas, mashed
  • 1½ cups self-rising flour
  • 1 can (14oz / 396g) sweetened condensed milk

Do this:

  1. Heat oven to 350°F (175°C). Grease a loaf pan.
  2. Mash the bananas in a bowl until mostly smooth.
  3. Stir in the condensed milk, then fold in the flour just until combined — don't overmix.
  4. Pour into the pan. Bake 50–60 minutes, until a toothpick comes out clean.
  5. Cool 10 minutes in the pan, then turn out onto a rack. Slice once it's fully cool.
