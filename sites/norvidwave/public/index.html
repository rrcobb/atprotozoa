norvidwave

an EP for @norvid-studies · charts, graphs & the grind

Synthesized live in your browser — no audio files, every note generated on play. Vocals by your device's speech synth. Hit play on a track, or play the whole thing.

headphones recommended · vocals use your browser's voice

Everything here is generated in the browser with the Web Audio API (oscillators, noise, envelopes — no samples or audio files) and the SpeechSynthesis API for the spoken vocals, so each track is composed on the fly when you press play. It's a toy homage, not a real release. Nothing is posted or collected. Best in a Chromium browser; speech voices vary by device.
