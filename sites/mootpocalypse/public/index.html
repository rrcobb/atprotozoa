🧟 mootpocalypse
every zombie is a real unliked post

the horde is your backlog

Somewhere out there, your moots posted things. You never liked them. Now they shamble.

Log in with Bluesky and mootpocalypse pulls your real mutuals, checks which of their recent posts you genuinely haven't liked, and drops every one of them into the town as a zombie wearing their avatar. Get close and press ❤️ LIKE to put one down — that's a real app.bsky.feed.like written straight to your own repo. No heart, no kill. Let too many corner you and it's game over.

Only scope requested: liking posts, and reading your moots' recent posts with your own login so it actually knows what you've liked. Nothing else is ever touched.
