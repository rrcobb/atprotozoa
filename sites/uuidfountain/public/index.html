is this a real problem?

No. UUIDv4 has 2^122 possible values — about 5.3 × 1036 of them. Even sustaining this fountain's rate forever, you would exhaust the space somewhere around 1023 years from now, several trillion times past the heat death of the universe. Every ID minted here is a real, cryptographically random crypto.randomUUID() call — it's just that "consuming" one does absolutely nothing to anyone else's supply. Spray freely.
