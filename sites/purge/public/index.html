purge · find your quiet mutuals

Mutuals — people you follow who follow you back — you never hear from. Paste a Bluesky handle and purge reads their follow graph to find mutuals, then checks whether each one has liked or replied to any of that account's posts recently. Anyone who hasn't gets listed, so you know who to consider unfollowing.

how this works
First, purge reads the target's getFollows and getFollowers in full and intersects them — that's the mutuals set. Then it pages the target's own recent posts via getAuthorFeed, stopping once a post falls outside the chosen window (up to 60 posts sampled), and for each one reads its public getLikes and direct getPostThread replies. Any mutual who shows up as a liker or direct replier on at least one sampled post counts as engaged; everyone else in the mutual set is "quiet" and lands on the purge list. Reposts don't count — the ask is specifically about liking or replying. Because this only samples the target's own posts (not every place the two accounts might interact — quote posts, other threads, DMs), a real friend who mostly engages elsewhere can still show up here; treat the list as a starting point for a look, not a verdict. If the account being checked hasn't posted at all in the chosen window, every mutual will look "quiet" for the trivial reason that there was nothing to engage with — purge flags that case instead of reporting it straight.
quiet mutual = follows each other, but no like or reply from them on any sampled post in the window. Reads Bluesky's public AppView (getFollows / getFollowers / getAuthorFeed / getLikes / getPostThread) — anonymous, everything client-side, no login. graph-crawl pattern copied from metamoots. idea from @psingletary.com. sibling of bisks.net.
