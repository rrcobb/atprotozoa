spoonerism

a bot that posts every spoonerism — or the closest thing to it

honest note: a bot that actually posts to Bluesky on a timer needs its own account and a stored app password — this build doesn't have one and can't wire one up. So instead: every spoonerism it would post is enumerated right here, each with its own permalink, plus a feed below that behaves like the bot's timeline would. One tap sends any of them to your own Bluesky post composer.
