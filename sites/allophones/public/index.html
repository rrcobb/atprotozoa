Loosely follows the real IPA pulmonic consonant chart (11 places × 8 manners) and the IPA vowel trapezoid, plus a nasalization toggle for the vowel. That inventory — — crossed with an optional onset and an optional coda gives distinct syllables. Each one's meaning is generated from its own phonetic features, not looked up in a list, so every single one of them has an answer.
