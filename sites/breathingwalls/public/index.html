breathing walls

A generative visual and sound piece built out of the imagery people reach for again and again in trip reports — walls that breathe, color that trails behind motion, pattern that crawls when you stop looking straight at it, time that stretches. Move your mouse or finger across it, turn the intensity up, click to send out a ripple.

What this is: a piece of generative web art — canvas animation, some math, a synthesized drone. What this isn't: a simulation of an actual drug, a dosing guide, or a synthesis recipe.
