claimstamp

Paste a Bluesky post URL below. A huge red stamp flies down and marks it CLAIM in bold letters — then, two seconds later, it shatters into pieces and catches fire. Nothing on Bluesky is ever touched; it's all local theater, right here in your browser.

what's actually happening

The post is read live via Bluesky's public, unauthenticated AppView (app.bsky.actor.getProfile to resolve the handle, then app.bsky.feed.getPosts for the record) — a plain browser fetch, no login, no server in between. Everything after that — the stamp drop, the impact shake, the shatter into shards, the fire — is CSS and JS animation on a local copy. The real post, out there on the network, is completely unaffected.
