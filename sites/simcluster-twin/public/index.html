how "ADVANCED ALGORITHMS" actually works

every account's recent posts get turned into a word-frequency vector (stopwords stripped), a hashtag set, an average post length / reply ratio / emoji rate, and a 24-bucket UTC hour-of-day histogram. your vector gets compared against each mutual's with cosine similarity on vocabulary and rhythm, Jaccard overlap on hashtags, and a normalized distance on posting style — weighted average of whichever of those four have enough data, 0–100. highest score is your twin, lowest is your anti-twin. it's real math. it is not a very deep model — two people who both post a lot about the same show will out-score two people who share a soul but never say #bluesky.
