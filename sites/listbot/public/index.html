listbot

Keep your own Bluesky lists by tagging.

Reply @listbot.bisks.net cool posters under someone's post and they join your list called "cool posters" — made for you if it doesn't exist yet. remove cool posters takes them off.

Or just say what you want: @listbot.bisks.net do it, make me a mute list for crypto spammers. It reads the thread and your existing lists, and asks if it can't tell.

Sign up

listbot can create and delete lists and list memberships. It cannot post as you, follow, or read your DMs.

Already signed up? see and edit your lists.

Whose call is a list

Yours, entirely. The lists live in your own repo, signed by your own account, and mean whatever you decide they mean. listbot never judges whether a tag is "right", because there's nobody for it to be right for.
