listbot

Keep your own Bluesky lists by tagging. Sign in once; after that you never come back here.

How it works

  1. Sign in below.
  2. See a post by someone you want on a list? Reply @listbot.bisks.net bots — or any list name you like.
  3. That post's author joins your list called "bots". If you don't have one, listbot makes it.
  4. @listbot.bisks.net remove bots takes them off again.

Bluesky already reads lists as mute lists, block lists, and feed curation lists, so a list is useful the moment it exists.

Sign in

listbot asks for exactly two permissions: create and delete lists, and create and delete list memberships. It cannot post, follow, or read your DMs.

Whose call is a list

Yours, entirely. The lists live in your own repo, signed by your own account, and mean whatever you decide they mean. listbot is a tool layer — it never judges whether a tag is "right", because there's nobody for it to be right for. If you put someone on a list called "bots" and they aren't a bot, that's between you and your list.

This is also why it's lists and not labels: a label is one labeler's signed assertion about someone, so it can't be per-person. A list can.

Plumbing

listbot holds an OAuth session so it can act on your PDS minutes after you tag it, with no browser open. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest with a key held as a Worker secret, separate from the storage itself. Signing out revokes it from Bluesky's own app settings.
