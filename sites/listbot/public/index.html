listbot

Reply to a post with a list name, and that post's author joins your list.

Sign up once. After that, @listbot.bisks.net cool posters under someone's post adds them to your list called "cool posters", made for you if you don't have one yet; remove cool posters takes them off. You never have to come back here.

You don't have to be terse. @listbot.bisks.net add them to my cool posters works too — it reads the thread and the lists you already have, so it uses the list you meant instead of making a near-duplicate, and asks when it can't tell.

Lists are curation lists by default — the kind that feed list-feeds and starter packs. Ask for a moderation list (make me a mute list for crypto spammers) if you want one people can mute or block from; whether to mute or block is each subscriber's choice, not a setting on the list.

listbot can create and delete lists and list memberships. It cannot post as you, follow, or read your DMs.

Already signed up? see and edit your lists.

Your lists, your call

The lists live in your own repo, signed by your own account. They mean whatever you decide they mean, and listbot never judges whether a tag is "right" — there's nobody for it to be right for.

Plumbing

listbot holds an OAuth session so it can act minutes after you tag it, with no browser open. Refresh tokens are encrypted at rest under a separate key. Revoke it any time from Bluesky's settings.

The part that reads your tag can only read — no write access, no credentials. It returns a decision and the server does the writing, so the person added is always the author of the post you replied to, never someone named in the tag.
