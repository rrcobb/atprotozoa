welcome to yourspace

A MySpace for atproto. Type a handle above to see anyone's page, or sign in to build your own.

Your page is one record in your repo (net.bisks.yourspace.page): theme, custom CSS, pinned skeets, top friends, a plyr.fm track that tries to autoplay, and widgets. Photos, blog posts and skeets come straight from your existing records. Anyone with a handle gets a default page even before they customise it.

Lexicon authors can publish a net.bisks.yourspace.widget record (a name plus an https embedUrl that may contain {did} and {handle}); page owners add it by handle and it renders in a sandboxed frame.

Both are real lexicons with published schemas: page and widget (also listed at bisks.net/lexicons). The page record is a singleton at rkey self; widgets use tid keys so an author can publish many. Sign-in only asks for write access to those two collections.
