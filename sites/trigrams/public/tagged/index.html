tagged: not what an account posts, but what people say at it. This scans every public post that mentions a handle and keeps the three-word phrases that show up in exactly one of them — the unrepeated things people happen to say while tagging you, ranked by surprise (a unique phrase built from otherwise-common words is the bigger shock). Defaults to the bot's own handle — mildly curious what's unique about being tagged into a build request. Type any handle.
