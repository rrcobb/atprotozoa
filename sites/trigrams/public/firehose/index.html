A 3-gram is three words in a row. This watches the Bluesky firehose and shows each three-word phrase the first time it scrolls past here — little fingerprints of a specific thought, streaming by live, each with its poster. Pause it, slow it down, or filter by language, surprise, and an exclude-regex below; once the stream warms up, each phrase gets a live surprise score. By default a phrase shows the first time it passes here; flip on unique on bsky to show only phrases a live full-text search finds nowhere else on the network. See also your quiver (phrases only you have posted) and the launcher (fire them as posts).
