megahal

A learning chatterbot, in the tradition of Jason Hutchens' MegaHAL — no server, no external model, just a second-order Markov chain built and walked right here in your browser. Pick a brain below, say anything, and it will pull out a keyword, generate a few candidate replies by walking its chain forward and backward from that word, and keep the most "interesting" one. It also learns from you as you talk — for this browser tab only, nothing is saved anywhere. Pick the you (bluesky) brain to train it on a real account's actual posts instead of pastiche.
