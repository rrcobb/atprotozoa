stillpoint

Three short guided meditations, narrated live by your browser and set to a generative ambient pad. Nothing downloads, nothing is recorded, nothing needs an account.

Before you start: this works best with sound on, in a spot where you can sit for a few minutes uninterrupted. Voice narration uses your browser's built-in text-to-speech — quality and available voices vary by browser, and captions are always shown too. This is a small relaxation tool, not therapy or medical advice — if you're in crisis, please reach out to a real person or a crisis line instead.
