relegation · who's getting dropped from your mutuals

League-table logic for your Bluesky mutuals. Sign in and it reads your whole post history in one shot, tallies every like and every direct reply anyone has ever given those posts, then builds a full standings table of your moots (mutual follows) — bottom of the table gets put in the relegation zone and paired one-for-one with the non-mutual followers who out-engaged them, as literal suggested swaps. Asked for by @psingletary.com.

suggested swaps 1:1

The bottom of your moots' table, paired one-for-one against the non-mutual followers who'd replace them best.

full standings

Every moot, ranked by total likes + replies given — the whole league table, not just the bottom of it.

least likes relegation zone

The moots who liked your posts the least — including zero times.

least replies relegation zone

The moots who replied to your posts the least — including never.

promotion candidates call-up

Followers you don't follow back, ranked by likes + replies given to your posts — the case for following them back.

"moots" are mutuals — the follow-back set. Likes and replies are counted straight off your own post history (every post you've ever made, not a sample) via the public AppView and Cerulea's backlink index — nothing is uploaded, this only reads.
