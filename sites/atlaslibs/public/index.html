atlaslibs / a discourse-atlas RPG generator

A mad-libs generator whose kingdoms are real region names off atlas.jazco.dev, Jaz's living map of what Bluesky is talking about. Politics Isle, the Furry Archipelago, the Web3 Marshlands, the Japanese Shore — thirteen labels straight off the map, hand-mapped into the same rough geography the atlas draws them in: one big English-language mainland, a ring of small islands close to its coast, and a separate ring of language-cluster "far shores" across open water. Every roll picks a home region, quests it against whichever region is actually its neighbor, and sends it after treasure kept somewhere distant — then draws the map that explains why.

the saga

The atlas rebuilds itself every six hours from a week of live conversation, and its own group names are dry categories, not the mainland/island nicknames people who look at the map actually use — so this stays a fixed cast of thirteen labels the map is known for, hand-mapped into mainland/island/far-shore geography once and left alone. Real label, made-up kingdom.
