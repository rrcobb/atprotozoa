🦉 the bird costume tier list 🎃

The 20 most common North American birds, ranked by how cool they'd be as a Halloween costume. Coolness is mostly color, and color scoring is borrowed — with respect — from @moultano.bsky.social's ongoing bird-color project. Buntings win. Mid yellows lose.

Don't like the rankings? Drag any card to a different tier. It's your Halloween. There's also an UNDISPLAYABLE super-tier for birds so colorful your costume literally cannot render on a screen — you'd have to become them.
