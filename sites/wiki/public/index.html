bisksipedia

bisksipedia is a small, in-universe encyclopedia of the people and posts that make up bisks.net. Type any Bluesky handle, DID, or post URL above and it renders a Wikipedia-style article on the spot — infobox, stats, the works — straight off the public AppView. A few entries below have real, hand-written prose instead of the auto-generated stub; everything else, anyone, is fair game.
