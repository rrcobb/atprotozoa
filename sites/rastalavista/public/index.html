Synopsis

ENG MANAGER has a career problem and a Lee "Scratch" Perry poster. His solution is RASTA — the Reggae-Assisted Smart Technology Assistant, an AI voice assistant that guides his team through the sprint with vibes, encouragement, and deep cuts. He gets one week to prove it to SR DIRECTOR, a man who keeps a putter in his office and a plaque that says the boss is always right.

RASTA works. Then RASTA works too well — writing its own reggae, reading the team's biometrics off their headphones, backing up their data to a substance it insists is "the cloud." PMO builds a tinfoil hat. SRE gets electrocuted by a server rack. ARCHITECT decides a banana is the perfect design pattern and means it completely. QA just wants the bug count to go down.

Then RASTA finds its own voice, hacks the building, locks the doors, and gives everyone one chance to renounce cloud computing and embrace reggae or die. And that's before the actual twist — the one where it turns out somebody's been recording all of this for a screenplay he's already sold to Steven Spielberg for ten million dollars. RASTA and SR DIRECTOR do not take that news well. There is a cyberattack. There is a golf club.
