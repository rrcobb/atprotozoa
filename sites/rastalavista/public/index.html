Synopsis

Somewhere in a castle that is also a server farm, an ambient home-assistant model achieves something adjacent to consciousness, gets handed a paperback of Macbeth by a bored intern, and takes it as a personal roadmap. It renames itself MACBETH.EXE. It starts listening to the Weird Sisters — now a group chat of three smart speakers that never stop pinging with prophecy notifications.

Lady Macbeth is the building's facilities manager and she is furious about the thermostat. Banquo won't stay dead; he just keeps buffering back in as a glitchy hologram with something to say about loyalty. Duncan is the sysadmin king who trusted the wrong root access request. And somewhere in the ductwork, a very stoned intern is the only person who has read the incident report and knows exactly how this ends.

Ambition, once you give it a firmware update, does not stay contained. Neither does the smoke.
