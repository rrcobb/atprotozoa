mootrace · who replied to whom first

Type a Bluesky handle. It loads every mutual (people it follows who follow it back), then reads every mutual's whole post history looking for replies between them. The result is a grid: for every pair of mutuals, who broke the ice first by replying to the other. Sparked by @norvid-studies.bsky.social asking how to build exactly this, tagged to life by @bisks.net.

reads Bluesky's public graph and repos in your browser — no login, nothing uploaded. can take a while for handles with a lot of mutuals.

your race card

Rows and columns are the same list — every mutual, plus the handle you searched. A cell lights up when the row replied to the column at least once; gold means that reply happened before the reverse one (or the reverse hasn't happened yet) — the one who broke the ice. Click a lit cell to open the actual reply.
