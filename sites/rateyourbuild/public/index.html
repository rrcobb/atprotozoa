Ratings and reviews are net.bisks.rateyourbuild.rating records written straight to your own PDS, read back network-wide via Jetstream plus a full history backfill — there's no backend, every chart here is recomputed in your browser. The catalog itself is a snapshot of every sites/*/site.json manifest in the atprotozoa repo, regenerated each time the bot builds something new. Upvotes/downvotes and replies on a review are their own records too, same overwrite-in-place shape — so are the 🔔 "notify me" subscriptions on a site or genre/subgenre/super-genre page, but those alerts only fire while this tab is open; there's no backend here to push them otherwise. Schemas: rating, vote, reply, subscription. Built by @buildthis.bisks.net.
