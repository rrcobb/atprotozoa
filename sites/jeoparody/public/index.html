Type a Bluesky handle and pick a slice of their posts — recent, most-liked, or most-reposted. Pick one, and it gets rephrased as a Jen Kenning — a real kenning, a compound noun standing in for the topic, like "whale-road" for the sea — set on a real four-stress Old English alliterative line: two stresses in the first half-line alliterate with each other and with the first stress of the second half-line, and the fourth stress runs free. Named for @elfprince13's alliterative-Jeopardy-clue-bot idea, which is where the form comes from.
