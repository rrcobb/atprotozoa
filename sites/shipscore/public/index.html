how does this work?

Each handle's public profile and last 100 posts-and-replies (app.bsky.actor.getProfile + app.bsky.feed.getAuthorFeed), plus the real follow relationship between them (app.bsky.graph.getRelationships), feed eight scored dimensions:

  • 🔗 already in orbit — do they already follow each other, one-way, or not at all
  • 🌙 timezone sync — overlap of what hour of day (UTC) they tend to post
  • 🌀 chaos compatibility — how similarly irregular their posting rhythm is
  • 💬 communication style — how close their reply-vs-post ratio is
  • 🎭 humor alignment — overlap of the actual emoji each of them uses
  • 📖 shared vocabulary — overlap of their most-used non-filler words, bio included
  • #️⃣ interest overlap — overlap of hashtags in recent posts
  • 🤝 acts of service — how similarly often they write real alt text on images

The headline score is a weighted average of all eight. The "love language" read scores five real ratios (exclamation rate, alt-text rate, reply rate, media rate, emoji rate) against a generous reference ceiling and reports the highest as each account's primary language — five numbers per account, not one coin flip. "Attachment style" is reply rate and posting-rhythm irregularity mapped onto four familiar-sounding labels, purely for flavor.

Nothing here reads private data, DMs, or likes, and nothing here claims to know who anyone actually has feelings for — it's a hypothetical "what if" read off public posting patterns, framed as pop psychology because that's more fun than a spreadsheet. Same two handles, same public data, always the same reading, until one of them posts or follows again.
