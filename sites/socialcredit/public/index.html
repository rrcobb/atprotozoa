Social Credit

a separate reputation layer from likes and reposts. sign in with Bluesky, paste anyone's handle, and give them a +1 or a -1 for their takes, their actions, their posts, their behavior — whatever. scores persist globally, keyed to DIDs so a handle change never loses history. or skip this site entirely — reply to their post with "socialcredit +1" or "socialcredit -1" and it counts the same vote, no sign-in here needed.
