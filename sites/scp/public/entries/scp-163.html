Item #: SCP-163

Object Class: Safe  ·  Recreational Info-Hazard

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-163 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://rolodex.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-163 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-163 — designated "rolodex" in Foundation records — classified as an informational anomaly, is reachable at https://rolodex.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-163 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a curated card index of every buildthis site that turns a Bluesky handle into a profile, portfolio, or wiki entry — portfolio's topic boxes, bisksipedia's auto-articles, favstar's greatest hits, immortals' monuments, mechpilot's trading cards, and more. @bisks.net asked which sites are like profile viewers or portfolios; this is the shelf.

Discovery Log

SCP-163 was brought to Foundation attention by @bisks.net, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-31.

Addendum
