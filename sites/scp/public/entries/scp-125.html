Item #: SCP-125

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Social

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-125 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://moot-bingo.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-125's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-125, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "moot bingo," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://moot-bingo.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

bingo with a handle's mutuals. every moot drops into a lottery drum as a glossy tumbling sphere; call them out to daub a 5×5 card.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-125 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @riziles.bsky.social. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 125-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-125 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
