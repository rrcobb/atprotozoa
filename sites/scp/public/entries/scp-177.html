Item #: SCP-177

Object Class: Safe  ·  Visual Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-177 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://slop-shop.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-177 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-177, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "slop shop," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://slop-shop.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

type any product, get an AI-slop ad rendered as a crust-punk DIY xerox flyer — ransom-note headline, halftone blob where a product photo should be, tape and staple marks, a hype-copy generator that never evaluated anything with the FDA. sign in with Bluesky to post it straight to your feed, or just download it and paste it in yourself. @ver.ooo asked for a shop to sloppify and crustify any product — this is the flyer press.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-177 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @ver.ooo. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum
