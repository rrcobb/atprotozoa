Item #: SCP-002

Object Class: Safe  ·  Grouped Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-002 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://activitygrid.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-002 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-002, catalogued internally as "activitygrid," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://activitygrid.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a GitHub-style contribution graph, but every green square is a day of bisks instead of commits. enter a handle, it paginates a year of their feed, buckets bisks by day, and draws the whole thing — streaks, busiest day, and all — as one downloadable, shareable image. @dave.9000ish.uk asked for a contribution heat map for a bsky poster's activity.

Discovery Log

SCP-002 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @dave.9000ish.uk, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum

Addendum 002-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-002 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
