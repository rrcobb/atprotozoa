Item #: SCP-092

Object Class: Safe  ·  Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-092 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://hellmole.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-092 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-092, catalogued internally as "hellmole," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://hellmole.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

generative demonic whack-a-mole. fiends claw up from a grid of summoning circles — each one drawn, named, colored and horned from scratch, never the same twice. click to banish for souls, build a combo, and whatever you do, spare the golden pilgrims. @ver.ooo asked for this one.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-092 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @ver.ooo. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 092-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-092 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
