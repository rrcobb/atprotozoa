Item #: SCP-132

Object Class: Safe  ·  Self-Replicating

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-132 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://mootstream.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-132 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-132, catalogued internally as "mootstream," classified as an aesthetic anomaly, is reachable at https://mootstream.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

wind currents drawn from Bluesky's live pulse — the firehose as weather.

Discovery Log

SCP-132 was brought to Foundation attention by @bisks.net, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum

Addendum 132-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-132 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
