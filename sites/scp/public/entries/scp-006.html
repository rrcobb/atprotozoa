Item #: SCP-006

Object Class: Safe  ·  Digital Visual

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-006 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://areyoumad.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-006 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-006 — designated "areyoumad" in Foundation records — classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://areyoumad.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

7778777.online asked for a checker that finds which of your moots haven't courtesy-liked your reply today. Type a handle: it pulls up everything you replied to today, checks who liked you back, and hands you a pre-written "are you mad at me?" post — one tap — for anyone who's gone quiet.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-006 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @7778777.online. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum
