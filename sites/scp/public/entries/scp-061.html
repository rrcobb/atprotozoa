Special Containment Procedures

SCP-061 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://didscope.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Keter-class object, SCP-061 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-061, catalogued internally as "didscope," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://didscope.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

astrology, but it's the last character of your <code>did:plc</code>. enter any Bluesky handle, it resolves the DID, and reads your fortune off whatever letter or digit the hash happened to end on — 32 signs, a cosmic rival, and one real recent post of yours pulled up as today's omen. @fromthewestmeadow.com asked for a site built from reading their whole feed — this riffs on a bit they'd posted twice about a DID-based horoscope.
