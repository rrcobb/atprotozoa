Item #: SCP-214

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-214 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://windmill.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-214's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-214, catalogued internally as "windmill," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://windmill.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

an economy-building game set on Animal Farm — sixteen seasons, split labor between the harvest and the windmill, sell grain at a floating market price. two modes: barter (no bank, no debt, growth capped by what you actually earn) or credit (Napoleon opens the Bank of Animal Farm, you set the interest rate yourself, and over-leverage risks a Frederick's-forged-banknotes-style crash). same farm, opposite rules — built to make the effect of credit and interest rates on a market something you feel, not just read about. @ver.ooo asked for an economy game demonstrating what credit and interest do to markets, reskinned as Animal Farm.

Discovery Log

SCP-214 was brought to Foundation attention by @ver.ooo, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 214-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-214 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
