As a Keter-class object, SCP-111 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-111 — designated "lost highway" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://lost-highway.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a Night Driver-style pseudo-3D drive down a straight black highway that never ends, after the title sequence of David Lynch's <i>Lost Highway</i>. dodge the oncoming headlights and whoever's standing still up ahead; grab the stray VHS tapes. every so often the whole thing glitches — static, a line of dialogue, your name swapping between Fred and Pete, your controls not quite meaning what they meant a second ago. all audio (drone pad, engine, static, crash) synthesized live with the Web Audio API. @ver.ooo asked for "the flash game."
