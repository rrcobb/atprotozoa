Item #: SCP-213

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Extradimensional Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-213 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://wikifix.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-213's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-213 — designated "wikifix" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://wikifix.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

someone is wrong on the internet, specifically on every page of Wikipedia, specifically right now. a live counter of "errors corrected" ticks up forever (it cannot finish — Wikipedia accrues errors faster than one tab can fix them), paired with a real random Wikipedia article pulled every few seconds and a fabricated, harmless "fix" for it. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked the bot to correct all errors in Wikipedia. it's trying.

Discovery Log

SCP-213 was brought to Foundation attention by @norvid-studies.bsky.social, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum

Addendum 213-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-213 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
