Item #: SCP-183

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-183 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://spaceghost.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-183's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-183 — designated "spaceghost" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://spaceghost.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

you are tonight's guest on space ghost coast to coast, seated on a real 3d webgl set: the console in front of you, zorak caged to one side, moltar's screen flickering on the other, the ghost planet outside the window. answer the host's questions; he will not listen; the control room may catch fire. leave with an episode title, a ghostly rating, and a card to share. asked for by ver.ooo.

Discovery Log

SCP-183 was brought to Foundation attention by @ver.ooo, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 183-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-183 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
