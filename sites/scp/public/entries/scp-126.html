Item #: SCP-126

Object Class: Safe  ·  Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-126 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://mootcraft.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-126 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-126, catalogued internally as "mootcraft," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://mootcraft.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a first-person voxel mine, real WebGL 3D. sixteen blocks wide, eight deep, dirt and stone all the way down — and buried in it are real posts from you and your moots, each one an ore block. dig one up and it pays out ore exactly equal to how many likes it has. @cee.wtf asked for "a 3d minecraft clone but every ore block is a post... and the number of likes... is how much ore it gives you."

Discovery Log

SCP-126 was brought to Foundation attention by @cee.wtf, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 126-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-126 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
