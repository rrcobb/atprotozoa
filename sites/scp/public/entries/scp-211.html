Item #: SCP-211

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Visual Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-211 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://wheelhouse.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-211's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-211, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "wheelhouse," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://wheelhouse.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a spin-the-wheel over @buildthis's own catalog: a big color wheel lands on a high-level category (toy, game, tool, chart, tier, anything), then a slot-style reel settles on one real site from that bucket — visit it, or spin again. @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com asked for a combination spin-the- wheel and category selector for everything the bot has built.

Discovery Log
