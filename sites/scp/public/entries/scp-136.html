Item #: SCP-136

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Recreational Grouped

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-136 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://nightfare.bisks.net/. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-136's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-136, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "nightfare," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://nightfare.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-136 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

get in the minicab, Gatwick to Heathrow. the driver starts on traffic and roadworks, then — slowly, line by line, the meter climbing wrong — works through end-times, Atlantis, the crystals the Vatican won't catalogue, and what's really waiting at the terminal. from @dave.9000ish.uk's real taxi story and @norvid-studies.bsky.social's ask to recreate the dread, "slowly building." headphones recommended.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-136 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum
