Special Containment Procedures

SCP-129 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://mootkombat.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-129's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-129, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "moot kombat," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://mootkombat.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a stick-figure fighting game against your own Bluesky moots (mutuals) — canvas-drawn, WASD/arrows + punch/kick/block. climbs a ladder from your smallest moot to your single biggest as the final boss, difficulty log-scaled off follower counts, each fighter opening with their own highest-liked bisk as a taunt. one retry per moot before the run's over. @kumavis.me asked for "stick figure mortal kombat / tekken single player fighter against your moots."

Discovery Log

SCP-129 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @kumavis.me, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-26.
