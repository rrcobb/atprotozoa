Item #: SCP-018

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Info-Hazard Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-018 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://bigredbutton.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-018's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-018, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "bigredbutton," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://bigredbutton.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-018 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

@ver.ooo asked @buildthis.bisks.net to build a site that tricks them into doing something dangerous. this is the safe version: six real dark patterns (fake urgency, a cancel button that dodges your cursor, pre-checked consent, confirmshaming, a forced countdown) stand between you and a big red button that was never wired to anything. press it anyway, then read what just got pulled on you.

Discovery Log
