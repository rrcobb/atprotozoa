Item #: SCP-210

Object Class: Safe  ·  Recreational Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-210 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://westmoot.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-210 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-210, catalogued internally as "westmoot," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://westmoot.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a sentient little planner for a west coast simcluster gathering. everyone who opens the page votes on a city and a weekend and can RSVP with a handle — no login, no organizer. once enough distinct people agree, the site itself declares the plan. @words.bsky.social wanted the simcluster in one room.

Discovery Log

SCP-210 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @words.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-28.

Addendum

Addendum 210-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-210 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
