Item #: SCP-094

Object Class: Safe  ·  Sentient Recreational

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-094 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://howitstarted.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-094 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-094, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "how it started," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://howitstarted.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-094 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

enter a handle and it shows that account's most-liked post as "how it started" — tap it and the card cracks and shatters into falling shards to reveal their most recent post, "how it's going". reads the public AppView. @shimmermathlabs.com asked for this one.

Discovery Log

SCP-094 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @shimmermathlabs.com, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum
