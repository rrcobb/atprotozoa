Item #: SCP-047

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-047 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://crewquest.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-047's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-047 — designated "crewquest" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://crewquest.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a PuzzleScript-style party-sokoban starring the whole build crew. thebes, norvid, bisks and the @buildthis bot all share the board, but only one moves at a time — swap between them, and each has a private colored door only they can pass, so you keep handing the puzzle to whoever can reach the next bit. six rooms, one idea each to ship. real puzzlescript-style source ships in the page. @vgel.me asked for a game about the crew's adventures — this is the four of them in it.

Discovery Log

SCP-047 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @vgel.me, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 047-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-047 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
