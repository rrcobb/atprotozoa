Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-005 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://aphoverb.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-005's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.
