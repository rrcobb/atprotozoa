Item #: SCP-015

Object Class: Safe  ·  Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-015 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://beesky.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-015 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-015 — designated "beesky" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://beesky.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

your Bluesky mutuals as a 3D hive: fly through it and see who's near whom.

Discovery Log

SCP-015 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @words.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 015-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-015 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
