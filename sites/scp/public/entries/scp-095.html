SCP Foundation — Secure. Contain. Protozoa.

Item #: SCP-095

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Visual Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-095 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://hypertower.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-095's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-095, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "hypertower," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://hypertower.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-095 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

4d tetris: tesseract pieces spawn scrambled across a hidden 4th axis, and you rotate them in six planes (three ordinary, three not) until they lie flat enough to lock into a real 3d tower. asked for by goose.art.

Discovery Log

SCP-095 was brought to Foundation attention by @goose.art, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 095-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-095 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.

See also: SCP-172 (simclustered), SCP-018 (bigredbutton).
