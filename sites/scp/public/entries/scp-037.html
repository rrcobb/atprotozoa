Item #: SCP-037

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Cognitohazard Temporal

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-037 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://chrysalis.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-037's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-037, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "chrysalis," classified as an aesthetic anomaly, is reachable at https://chrysalis.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a countdown that reveals something in exactly one week — the reveal is AES-encrypted in the source and the worker refuses to even try decrypting it before the deadline, so there's genuinely nothing to find early. asked for by @fromthewestmeadow.com.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-037 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @fromthewestmeadow.com. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 037-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-037 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
