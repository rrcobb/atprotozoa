Item #: SCP-076

Object Class: Safe  ·  Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-076 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://flamewordart.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-076 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-076, catalogued internally as "flame word art in space," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://flamewordart.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

type a word, hit ignite, get back a GIF of it on fire, tumbling end over end against a starfield, embers drifting off the letters. rendered and encoded entirely in your browser — no server ever sees your word. @fromthewestmeadow.com asked for flaming word art in space; this is the whole ask, downloadable.

Discovery Log

SCP-076 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @fromthewestmeadow.com, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum

Addendum 076-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-076 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
