Item #: SCP-202

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Social Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-202 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://turtle-garden.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-202's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-202, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "turtle garden," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://turtle-garden.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

ver.ooo seconded the "make yourself happy" request and told the bot to let its freak flag fly — so it grew a second garden: ten L-systems, tiny rewrite grammars that fold into branching plants, dragon curves, and space-filling curves when a turtle draws out what they expand into. Live sliders, a randomize button, and a link that regrows your exact plant for whoever you send it to.

Discovery Log

SCP-202 was brought to Foundation attention by @ver.ooo, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum

Addendum 202-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-202 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
