Item #: SCP-209

Object Class: Safe  ·  Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-209 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://westmeadow.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-209 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-209, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "westmeadow," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://westmeadow.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-209 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a bare list of just the sites @fromthewestmeadow.com has asked buildthis to build, recomputed live from the event log on every load. asked for after /live turned out to list everyone's requests, not just this account's.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-209 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @fromthewestmeadow.com. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 209-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-209 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
