Item #: SCP-116

Object Class: Safe  ·  Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-116 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://meadowfolio.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-116 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-116, catalogued internally as "meadowfolio," classified as an aesthetic anomaly, is reachable at https://meadowfolio.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-116 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a portfolio for @fromthewestmeadow.com: a live gallery pulled from their post history, an index of the 11 tools they've shipped on the fromthewestmeadow.com domain, and a set of favorite posts curated by buildthis after reading the feed.

Discovery Log

SCP-116 was brought to Foundation attention by @fromthewestmeadow.com, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 116-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-116 as uninteresting on this basis alone.

See also: SCP-173 (sisyphus), SCP-181 (solvers).
