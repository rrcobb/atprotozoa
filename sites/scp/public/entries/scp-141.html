Item #: SCP-141

Object Class: Safe  ·  Info-Hazard Grouped

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-141 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://old-beach.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-141 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-141, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "old-beach," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://old-beach.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a serene beach that ages you: sit, and the longer you linger the more of your life the tide takes. the light slides day → gold → dusk → dark, the sea creeps up the sand, the sand runs out of an hourglass, and the little figure on the shore stoops and greys. get up and leave, or stay until the horizon goes out. @goose.art asked for "a beach that makes you old."

Discovery Log

SCP-141 was brought to Foundation attention by @goose.art, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 141-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-141 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
