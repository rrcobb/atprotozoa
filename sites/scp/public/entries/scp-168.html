Item #: SCP-168

Object Class: Safe  ·  Self-Replicating Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-168 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://simclash.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-168 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-168, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "simclash," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://simclash.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a generative visual metaphor for SimClusters: two glowing swarms of dots drift together, spark gold wherever they overlap, then drift apart and reform — forever. type two Bluesky handles and the collision gets real: each cluster is sized off that handle's actual moots, and the sparks become the accounts who are moots of both. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked for "a visual metaphor for simclusters colliding" (tagged by @cee.wtf).

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-168 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 168-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-168 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
