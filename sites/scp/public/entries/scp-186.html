an eyeball test in four rounds: bisect a line, click the dot dead-center of a grid, guess how many beans are in the jar, then drag a box over exactly the right number of them. scored out of 100, with a title to match. @cee.wtf asked for something that "tests the users on quick spatial and counting based tasks."
