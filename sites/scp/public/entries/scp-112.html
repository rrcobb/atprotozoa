Item #: SCP-112

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Recreational

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-112 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://mahjong-solitaire.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-112's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-112, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "mahjong solitaire," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://mahjong-solitaire.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

an accessible mahjong pairs game: tap two matching tiles to clear them, no dragging, no timer. free tiles get a blue outline and lift up; covered or boxed-in ones sit flat and dim, so the stacking is always legible at a glance. undo and restart live in the toolbar, and a "step down a level" button is one tap away the moment a board runs out of moves. four difficulty levels, guaranteed-solvable deals, big tap targets throughout. @heartpunk.com asked for something aimed at intense accessibility for older users on older phones — this is that.
