Item #: SCP-096

Object Class: Keter  ·  Self-Replicating

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-096 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://idea-island.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Keter-class object, SCP-096 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-096, catalogued internally as "idea-island," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://idea-island.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

an idea that lands. an idea with land. a landed idea. a little island in a calm night sea, and a lightbulb hovering over it. click the water and the idea lands — the bulb flickers on, the island glows warm, and ripples spread out across the dark. a quiet ambient loop that also lights itself now and then. @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com asked for this one (echoed by @notharlock.poast.ing).

Discovery Log

SCP-096 was brought to Foundation attention by @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 096-A: SCP-096's Keter designation reflects containment difficulty, not necessarily hostility. No harmful behavior has been documented at time of writing.
