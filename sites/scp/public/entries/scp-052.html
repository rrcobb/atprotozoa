Item #: SCP-052

Object Class: Safe  ·  Info-Hazard Digital

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-052 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://declined.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-052 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-052, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "declined," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://declined.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

the build bot's evil twin. type an idea, hit build, and it always says no — with a fresh over-elaborate excuse every time. the moon is in the wrong phase; it violates the third law of website-dynamics; parking's full. a running tally of everything it's refused.

Discovery Log

SCP-052 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @antiali.as, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 052-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-052 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
