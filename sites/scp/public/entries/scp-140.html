Item #: SCP-140

Object Class: Safe  ·  Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-140 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://oblique.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-140 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-140, catalogued internally as "oblique," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://oblique.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-140 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

Eno & Schmidt's Oblique Strategies — a deck of cryptic cards for unblocking a stuck creative session — redrawn one card at a time for a different collaborator: a programming agent mid-task. original cards, same terse register. tap to draw the next one.

Discovery Log

SCP-140 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum

Addendum 140-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-140 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
