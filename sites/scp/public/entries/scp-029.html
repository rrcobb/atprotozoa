Item #: SCP-029

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Memetic Temporal

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-029 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://cancelfutures.bisks.net/. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-029's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-029, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "cancelfutures," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://cancelfutures.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a mock prediction market on which bluesky discourse cycle happens next — mad-libs archetypes (a tradwife influencer who found bluesky, an AI art account, your mutual who unfollows and refollows) crossed with a callout template and priced with odds. bet play money, watch the pot resolve, no real people, no real money. asked for by @dollspace.gay.

Discovery Log

SCP-029 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @dollspace.gay, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 029-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-029 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
