Item #: SCP-160

Object Class: Safe  ·  Sentient

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-160 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://quotehof.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-160 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-160, catalogued internally as "quotehof," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://quotehof.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

the quote poster's hall of fame — but only one shape counts: a Quote Post that's just an image, quoting a post that's just text. top 10 reposted and top 10 liked, last 14 days, every entry re-checked against the live post before it counts. no wammies. idea from @psingletary.com, tagged in by @antiali.as.

Discovery Log

SCP-160 was brought to Foundation attention by @psingletary.com, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum
