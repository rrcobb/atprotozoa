Item #: SCP-057

Object Class: Safe  ·  Grouped

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-057 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://desertbus.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-057 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-057 — designated "desert bus" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://desertbus.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

the famous 8-hour dead-straight drive, compressed into a 30-second fuel tank — speed is tied directly to fuel left, so the joke deceleration is the whole run. once the tank drops below half, Grace starts sending fuel warnings from the dashboard every 5 seconds, and none of them go away on their own — close them or let them cascade across the whole screen while you try to steer. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked for it, quoting a gracekind.net exchange ("less fuel = less car velocity. exactly.").

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-057 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-28.
