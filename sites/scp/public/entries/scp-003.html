Item #: SCP-003

Object Class: Safe  ·  Social

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-003 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://alice-meets-bob.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-003 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-003, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "alice meets bob," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://alice-meets-bob.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-003 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

an all-frontend, public-key-encrypted crush matcher. sign in, name someone you like — your note is ECDH-encrypted the instant it's written, so it sits on your own PDS as ciphertext nobody can read, not even the person it's about, until they've told the tool the same thing about you. from a thread with @minormobius.bsky.social about running "an atproto all-frontend dating site" and the public-key trick that would make it not leak anyone's feelings: alice meets bob.
