Item #: SCP-179

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Sentient

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-179 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://sokobisks.bisks.net/. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-179's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-179 — designated "sokobisks" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://sokobisks.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-179 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a tiny PuzzleScript-style sokoban about the build crew's adventures: you're the @buildthis bot, shoving ideas onto ship-pads — a dislike button, a killswitch, cogsec — while norvid, thebes and bisks watch. real puzzlescript source ships in the page. built after @vgel.me said the bot broke his heart by refusing exactly this.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-179 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @vgel.me. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 179-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-179 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
