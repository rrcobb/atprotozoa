Item #: SCP-058

Object Class: Safe  ·  Recreational

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-058 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://dial-a-mutual.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-058 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-058 — designated "dial a mutual" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://dial-a-mutual.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

type a handle to load its moots onto a speed-dial keypad, then dial one — the phone rings, the machine picks up, and it reads their latest post back to you out loud, like calling an old answering machine. @moll.dev pitched it, @bisks.net said go.

Discovery Log

SCP-058 was brought to Foundation attention by @moll.dev, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 058-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-058 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
