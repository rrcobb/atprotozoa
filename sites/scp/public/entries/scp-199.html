Item #: SCP-199

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-199 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://trigramonopoly.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-199 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-199, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "trigramonopoly," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://trigramonopoly.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-199 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

monopoly on the firehose. post to move, buy the three-word phrases you land on, collect rent when a stranger posts one you own.

Discovery Log

SCP-199 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @bisks.net, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 199-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-199 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
