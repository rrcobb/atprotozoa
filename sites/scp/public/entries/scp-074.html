Item #: SCP-074

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Ideological

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-074 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://fitzcarraldo.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-074's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-074 — designated "fitzcarraldo" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://fitzcarraldo.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a 2001-vintage Yahoo! web portal built around one exhibit: an 8-bit haul of Fitzcarraldo's steamship over the mountain, mash SPACE to work the capstan. Herzog directs with dry one-liners, Kinski's tantrums spike a chaos meter that can shut the shoot down cold, and Wim Wenders logs the whole thing live to the sidebar as "Burden of Dreams." @ver.ooo asked for it in full Nintendo fidelity, then tagged the bot again to turn up the chaos.

Discovery Log

SCP-074 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @ver.ooo, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum

Addendum 074-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-074 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
