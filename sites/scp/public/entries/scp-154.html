Item #: SCP-154

Object Class: Safe  ·  Memetic

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-154 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://prosodle.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-154 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-154 — designated "prosodle" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://prosodle.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a wordle-like where you roll the 9 letters of MINECRAFT and get scored on cadence, mouthfeel, and punch. grew out of @shibbi.me and @aiun.bsky.social trading anagrams (crinemaft, mincefart, mantrefic...) in a reply thread — the bot made it a daily 3-roll game.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-154 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @shibbi.me. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 154-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-154 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
