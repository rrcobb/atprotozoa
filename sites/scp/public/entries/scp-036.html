SCP Foundation — Secure. Contain. Protozoa.

Item #: SCP-036

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-036 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://chironhell.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-036 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-036, catalogued internally as "chironhell," classified as an aesthetic anomaly, is reachable at https://chironhell.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

the live Bluesky firehose with nothing left but the ticker: every post that flies past becomes a text chiron, sized and angled at random, crossing the screen in every direction at once, layered on top of each other forever. only text, no cards, no avatars — pure maximalist noise. @words.bsky.social asked for "chirons chirons chirons."

Discovery Log

SCP-036 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @words.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 036-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-036 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
