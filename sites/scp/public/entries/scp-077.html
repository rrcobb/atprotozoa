Special Containment Procedures

SCP-077 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://fourk.bisks.net/. As a Thaumiel-class object, SCP-077 has been assessed as useful to Foundation operations and is retained in active service rather than mere containment.

Description

SCP-077 — designated "fourk" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://fourk.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-077 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a raymarched neon fractal tunnel, hand-rolled in about 4.8kb of plain JavaScript plus a GLSL shader — no libraries, no build step. drag or swipe to steer between the pillars as they spiral past. the page measures its own script and shows you the real byte count. @jmoonz.bsky.social asked for a "cool 4kb js webgl webpage demo" that'd make them go "wow!"

Discovery Log
