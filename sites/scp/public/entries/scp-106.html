Item #: SCP-106

Object Class: Safe  ·  Grouped Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-106 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://lasercats.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-106 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-106, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "lasercats," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://lasercats.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a whimsical 2:09 animated music video: a cat chases a laser dot, stares out a window at birds, and asserts its autonomy by ignoring the human that feeds it — scored with a fully synthesized chiptune loop, since the real song's link arrived truncated. @jmsdnns.bsky.social pitched it, @bisks.net said let's have it.

Discovery Log

SCP-106 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @jmsdnns.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 106-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-106 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
