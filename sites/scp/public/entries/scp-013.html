Item #: SCP-013

Object Class: Safe  ·  Auditory Info-Hazard

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-013 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://beanjar.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-013 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-013, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "🫘 jar of beans," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://beanjar.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a jar of beans you can shake — real physics, real sound, no purpose whatsoever.

Discovery Log

SCP-013 was brought to Foundation attention by @cee.wtf, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum

Addendum 013-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-013 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
