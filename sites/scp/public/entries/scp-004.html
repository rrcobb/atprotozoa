Item #: SCP-004

Object Class: Safe  ·  Ideological Info-Hazard

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-004 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://alignment-chart.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-004 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-004, catalogued internally as "network alignment chart," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://alignment-chart.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

drop a handle's mutuals onto a 3×3 D&D alignment grid — chill↔harsh × gooning↔mogging — by vibe-checking their recent posts. find yourself, find your worst moot. it's a bit.

Discovery Log

SCP-004 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @antiali.as, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum

Addendum 004-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-004 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
