Description

SCP-089 — designated "hashclaim" in Foundation records — classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://hashclaim.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

someone posts a hash today, then posts the "source" tomorrow and claims it checks out. paste both in and hashclaim recomputes it in your browser — SHA-1/256/384/512 or MD5, auto-detected — and tells you whether the claim actually holds up.
