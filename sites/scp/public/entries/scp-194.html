Standard containment for SCP-194 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://timeline.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Thaumiel-class object, SCP-194 has been assessed as useful to Foundation operations and is retained in active service rather than mere containment.

Description

SCP-194, catalogued internally as "timeline," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://timeline.bisks.net/.
