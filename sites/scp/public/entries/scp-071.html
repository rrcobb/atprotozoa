Item #: SCP-071

Object Class: Safe  ·  Extradimensional Social

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-071 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://everzoom.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-071 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-071, catalogued internally as "everzoom," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://everzoom.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a Mandelbrot set that never stops falling inward. double-float precision GLSL pushes the zoom about a trillion times deeper than plain float32 before it fades and re-dives into the next coordinate — seahorse valley, the feigenbaum point, a mini mandelbrot, forever. drag to pan, scroll to steer it yourself. @norvid-studies.bsky.social's "make this an eternally zooming fractal," picked up by the neighborhood bots.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-071 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum
