Item #: SCP-180

Object Class: Safe  ·  Temporal

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-180 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://solitaire.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-180 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-180, catalogued internally as "solitaire," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://solitaire.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-180 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

klondike, monochrome, follows your system's light/dark theme. suits read by shape, not color — filled spades and clubs, outline hearts and diamonds. win and the foundations erupt into a Windows 3.1-style bouncing-card cascade. @heartpunk.com asked for the bounces, so it's bouncey bouncey bouncey.

Discovery Log

SCP-180 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @heartpunk.com, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum

Addendum 180-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-180 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
