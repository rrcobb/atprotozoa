Item #: SCP-097

Object Class: Safe  ·  Grouped Visual

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-097 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://idea-mill.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-097 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-097, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "idea-mill," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://idea-mill.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a mill that never stops grinding out website ideas for the build bot. a fresh one drops every few seconds; tap “build it” to tag @buildthis with it, pre-filled, and set this whole thing spinning again.

Discovery Log

SCP-097 was brought to Foundation attention by @gracekind.net, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 097-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-097 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
