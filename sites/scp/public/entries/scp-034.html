Item #: SCP-034

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-034 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://change.bisks.net/. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-034's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-034 — designated "change!" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://change.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-034 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a port of GamesCrafters' <em>Change!</em> — a 14-point sliding board where 3 pieces a side slide forward-only, win by occupying the opponent's home or trapping them. SVG board, Tokyo Night colors, fits a phone screen, and a bot that exhaustively solves the whole game tree before it makes its first move (turns out blue, who moves first, is a forced loss under perfect play). @yonderdavid.bsky.social asked for the board; @bisks.net said go.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-034 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @yonderdavid.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-26.
