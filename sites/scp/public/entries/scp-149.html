Item #: SCP-149

Object Class: Safe  ·  Memetic Self-Replicating

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-149 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://platoscave.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-149 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-149, catalogued internally as "plato's cave," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://platoscave.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-149 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a Y2K flash-portal simulation of the Allegory of the Cave, exactly as p4ck3th4ck3r would have shipped it in 2002: guess the shadow puppets while chained to the wall, snap your chain and mash your way up the tunnel, get blinded by the Forms, then climb back down to tell the others — who throw rocks at you for your trouble. @ver.ooo asked for this one.

Discovery Log

SCP-149 was brought to Foundation attention by @ver.ooo, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-27.

Addendum

Addendum 149-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-149 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
