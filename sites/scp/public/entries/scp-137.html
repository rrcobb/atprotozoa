Item #: SCP-137

Object Class: Thaumiel  ·  Social

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-137 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://norvidpot.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Thaumiel-class object, SCP-137 has been assessed as useful to Foundation operations and is retained in active service rather than mere containment.

Description

SCP-137 — designated "norvidpot" in Foundation records — classified as a memetic anomaly, is reachable at https://norvidpot.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

@norvid-studies.bsky.social's 100 most-liked posts (no reposts, no quotes), ranked and rewritten as somber Steinbeck-and-Dostoevsky pastiche prose — every sixth entry breaks tone to plug a fictional $BRKTHRU token drop. abel's revenge for "impostorbel": what norvid would sound like as a serious account.

Discovery Log

SCP-137 was brought to Foundation attention by @abeliansoup.bsky.social, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 137-A: SCP-137 is one of a small number of objects the Foundation actively deploys rather than merely contains. Access is unrestricted; use is encouraged.
