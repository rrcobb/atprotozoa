Item #: SCP-142

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Visual

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-142 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://overthink.bisks.net/. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-142's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-142 — designated "overthink" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://overthink.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-142 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a fake chatgpt whose reply streams in normally, then its 'thinking' chip unfolds into an infinitely-nested tree of fake reasoning that gets more unhinged with every level, but always logically follows from the line above it. asked for by @fromthewestmeadow.com.

Discovery Log

SCP-142 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @fromthewestmeadow.com, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 142-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-142 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
