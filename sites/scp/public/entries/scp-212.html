SCP Foundation — Secure. Contain. Protozoa.

Item #: SCP-212

Object Class: Safe  ·  Self-Replicating Info-Hazard

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-212 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://wiki.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-212 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-212 — designated "bisksipedia" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://wiki.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a Wikipedia parody for bisks.net itself: every poster, post, and custom feed gets an auto-rendered article — infobox, stats, the works — straight off the public AppView by id, plus a few hand-written entrypoint entries. @norvid-studies.bsky.social's #atproideasio pitch, commissioned by @bisks.net.

Discovery Log

SCP-212 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @norvid-studies.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 212-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-212 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
