Item #: SCP-060

Object Class: Safe  ·  Ideological

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-060 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://didrank.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-060 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-060, catalogued internally as "didrank," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://didrank.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a live leaderboard of the most-frequently-posting Bluesky DIDs, over rolling windows from the last minute out to all-time — plus special date-specific views for New Year's Eve, Valentine's Day and Christmas Day. watches the Jetstream firehose directly, no canned data. built for @fromthewestmeadow.com.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-060 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @fromthewestmeadow.com. First logged 2026-08-01.

Addendum

Addendum 060-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-060 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
