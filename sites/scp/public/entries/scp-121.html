Description

SCP-121, catalogued internally as "misaligned," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://misaligned.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a found-poetry generator for fictional rogue-AI "directives" — short, redacted-memo verses that are half confession, half threat, half poem, assembled client-side from a word bank. every directive gets a fake classification, a made-up AI signature, and a self-reported alignment score. nothing here talks to a real model; it's a template wearing a trenchcoat. @words.bsky.social asked for something mischievous, nefarious, or poetically misaligned. this tried to be all three.
