Item #: SCP-178

Object Class: Keter  ·  Extradimensional Recreational

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-178 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://slopwater.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Keter-class object, SCP-178 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-178, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "slop water," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://slopwater.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

dunk the powder in, shake the bottle for your life against a 7-second clock, get a custom sludge flavor and a purity score built from how many times and how evenly you shook it. ends in a bottle wrapper worth reviewing, generated just for you. @norvid-studies.bsky.social's thread about water bottle wrapper designs and dunking Huel into Icelandic Glacial, turned into something to shake.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-178 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 178-A: SCP-178's Keter designation reflects containment difficulty, not necessarily hostility. No harmful behavior has been documented at time of writing.
