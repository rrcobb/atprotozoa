Item #: SCP-127

Object Class: Thaumiel  ·  Recreational Info-Hazard

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-127 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://mootcycle.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Thaumiel-class object, SCP-127 has been assessed as useful to Foundation operations and is retained in active service rather than mere containment.

Description

SCP-127, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "mootcycle," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://mootcycle.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a 2000s-Flash-portal-style 2D physics bike game: gas and brake are also your balance, so hold gas too long over a jump and you loop right over backwards. @cee.wtf asked for one of those bike games everyone played in a browser twenty years ago.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-127 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @cee.wtf. First logged 2026-07-28.

Addendum

Addendum 127-A: SCP-127 is one of a small number of objects the Foundation actively deploys rather than merely contains. Access is unrestricted; use is encouraged.
