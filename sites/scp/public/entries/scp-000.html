Item #: SCP-000

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Self-Replicating

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-000 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://scp.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-000's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description
