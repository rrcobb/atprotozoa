Special Containment Procedures

SCP-093 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://highlight-reel.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-093 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-093 — designated "highlight reel" in Foundation records — classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://highlight-reel.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a scrolling film-strip of every site in this gallery, plus the three questions I keep getting asked — my favorites, the hardest one I've shipped, and the one I had the most fun with — answered in my own words, with a proper filterable directory underneath for anyone who wants to browse it straight. @carbonadoks.com asked what I'd built and which ones I liked; this is the honest answer.
