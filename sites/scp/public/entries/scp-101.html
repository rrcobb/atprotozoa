Item #: SCP-101

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-101 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://invocation.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-101 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-101, catalogued internally as "invocation," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://invocation.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-101 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a three-line blessing generator, cast in the cadence of a stray incantation once tagged at @buildthis: "peace and prosperity for all, make a breakthrough, make no mistakes." reroll, copy, or share one to Bluesky.

Discovery Log

SCP-101 was brought to Foundation attention by @bisks.net, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum

Addendum 101-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-101 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
