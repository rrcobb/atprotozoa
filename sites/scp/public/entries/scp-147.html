Item #: SCP-147

Object Class: Safe  ·  Auditory Visual

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-147 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://pixel-fishing.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-147 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-147 — designated "pixel fishing" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://pixel-fishing.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

cast into a pixel pond that runs its own day/dusk/night/dawn cycle. watch the bobber, click fast when it dips to hook the bite, then tap "reel!" to fight the tension bar before the line snaps. eight hand-pixeled fish — one only bites after dark, one's a vanishingly rare golden carp — plus the odd boot or tin can, and a field guide that fills in as you catch. @steve-adams.me asked for a pixel fishing game; @bisks.net gave it the go-ahead in-thread.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-147 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @steve-adams.me. First logged 2026-07-25.

Addendum
