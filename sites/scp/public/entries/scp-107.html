Item #: SCP-107

Object Class: Thaumiel  ·  Digital

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-107 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://lavalamp.bisks.net/. As a Thaumiel-class object, SCP-107 has been assessed as useful to Foundation operations and is retained in active service rather than mere containment.

Description

SCP-107, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "lava lamp," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://lavalamp.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

enter a handle and a dozen or so of their posts drift inside a lava lamp — the more likes a post got, the higher it floats, the fewer it got, the lower it sinks. a random mix of bangers and total flops, first few words peeking past each blob, cycling out for fresh picks every few seconds. @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com asked for a "skeet lava lamp."

Discovery Log

SCP-107 was brought to Foundation attention by @catblanketflower.yuwakisa.com, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 107-A: SCP-107 is one of a small number of objects the Foundation actively deploys rather than merely contains. Access is unrestricted; use is encouraged.
