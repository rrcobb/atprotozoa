Item #: SCP-197

Object Class: Safe  ·  Visual Recreational

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-197 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://toroidarium.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-197 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-197 — designated "toroidarium" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://toroidarium.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-197 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

enter a Bluesky handle and its moots (mutuals) become fish in a wraparound aquarium — no walls, swim off the right edge and re-enter on the left, off the bottom and back in the top. feed them, click one to see who it is. @nequals001.bsky.social asked for a site built from reading all their posts; @bisks.net read the room and built a torus.

Discovery Log

SCP-197 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @nequals001.bsky.social, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 197-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-197 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
