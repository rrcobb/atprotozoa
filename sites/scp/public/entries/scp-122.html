Item #: SCP-122

Object Class: Safe  ·  Temporal Digital

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-122 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://mistake.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-122 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-122, catalogued internally as "mistake," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://mistake.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a daily "make a mistake" assignment: type what you'd like to mess up (or leave it blank) and get today's mistake — a severity from harmless to legendary and a line to go do it, seeded from the date so asking twice today gives the same one. @demigirlboss.bsky.social tagged the bot with exactly three words — "make a mistake" — so that's the whole brief.

Discovery Log

SCP-122 was brought to Foundation attention by @demigirlboss.bsky.social, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 122-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-122 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
