Item #: SCP-144

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Extradimensional Social

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-144 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://padmoot.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-144's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-144 — designated "padmoot" in Foundation records — classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://padmoot.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a social synthesizer / beat pad on atproto. finger-drum on an MPC grid, a Launchpad session grid, or a TR-909 strip — same beat underneath, switchable mid-session. save your pattern straight to your own PDS as a custom lexicon record, export it as JSON/WAV to disk, or jam on a moot's shared pattern. @erisianrite.com asked for a social beat pad with a selectable controller layout.
