Item #: SCP-068

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Sentient

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-068 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://epistemics.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-068's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-068, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "epistemics," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://epistemics.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

enter a Bluesky handle and it reads their recent posts and replies back, cross-referencing every stance they've taken for reversals, "always" vs "never," a fog of hedging, and unearned "obviously"s — a docket of epistemic sins, quoted and linked. no LLM behind it, just a very literal-minded grep — from @norvid-studies.bsky.social's "prolog for beliefs" idea, tagged in by @agnoster.net asking to hold a mirror up to our words.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-068 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @agnoster.net. First logged 2026-07-29.

Addendum

Addendum 068-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-068 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
