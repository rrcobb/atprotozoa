Item #: SCP-120

Object Class: Safe  ·  Recreational Ideological

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-120 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://mirrormode.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-120 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-120 — designated "mirrormode" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://mirrormode.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

your phone's front camera is a wide-angle lens shot from eight inches away; your bathroom mirror is neither. drop a photo in (or use your camera), drag a 24mm-135mm focal-length slider, and it re-renders your face with a radial warp — bulged and selfie-distorted at the wide end, flattened and mirror-flattering at the long end — plus a mirror-flip and a warm soft-focus grade. entirely client-side, nothing ever uploaded. @7778777.online asked for a service that makes your photo look as good as your bathroom mirror does.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-120 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @7778777.online. First logged 2026-07-28.

Addendum

Addendum 120-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-120 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
