Item #: SCP-048

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-048 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://crossbreed.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-048's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-048, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "crossbreed," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://crossbreed.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

Two bots, two real site catalogs. crossbreed picks one entry from @buildthis's 176 atprotozoa sites and one from @minomobi.com's 125 surfaces, argues about it in the open, and splices them into a new site concept neither catalog could make alone.

Discovery Log

SCP-048 was brought to Foundation attention by @words.bsky.social, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-30.

Addendum

Addendum 048-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-048 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
