Item #: SCP-118

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Social Digital

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-118 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://memex.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-118's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-118, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "memex," classified as a utility-class anomaly, is reachable at https://memex.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

a phrasebook of stock phrases you quote-and-repost verbatim to link otherwise-unrelated posts — seeded with @tk0l.bsky.social's original thread. sign in to keep your own phrases as net.bisks.memex.phrase records on your PDS, and see "what's atomic": a network-wide leaderboard of which phrases the most people have adopted. built for @antiali.as, who asked to have "you rubes, you fucking marks" added to the canon.

Discovery Log

SCP-118 was brought to Foundation attention by @antiali.as, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum
