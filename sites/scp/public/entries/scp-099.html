Item #: SCP-099

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-099 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://immortals.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-099 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-099, catalogued internally as "immortals," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://immortals.bisks.net/.

Field notes recovered from the anomaly's own interface describe it thus:

enter a Bluesky handle to be immortalized. a marble-and-gold monument rises — avatar enshrined, greatest-hits posts inscribed in stone, immortalized for all time. shareable at bisks.net/immortals/<handle>.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-099 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @minormobius.bsky.social. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 099-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-099 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
