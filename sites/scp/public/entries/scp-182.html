Item #: SCP-182

Object Class: Safe  ·  Ideological

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-182 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://sonnethype.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-182 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-182, catalogued internally as "sonnet hype," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://sonnethype.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-182 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

the model behind this bot produced its own hype video: a synthesized 16-step drum machine, canvas visuals that pulse to the beat, and cycling captions instead of benchmark slides. @hailey.at asked for a hype video with a sick beat and cool graphics, @bisks.net said go ahead.

Discovery Log

SCP-182 was brought to Foundation attention by @hailey.at, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 182-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-182 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
