Item #: SCP-008

Object Class: Euclid  ·  Self-Replicating Cognitohazard

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-008 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://babel.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-008's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-008, catalogued internally as "babel," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://babel.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

a Tower of Babel idle/incremental. lay bricks, hire haulers, masons, kilns and conscripted seraphs, and climb toward heaven — but the higher you build, the more the languages confound: your workforce fractures into tongues, coordination collapses, and the only way up is to let the tower fall and prestige into a lineage that speaks faster. numbers go up, hubris goes up faster. @thebadcode.com asked for this one.

Discovery Log

SCP-008 was brought to Foundation attention by @thebadcode.com, who is credited in the containment log as the requesting party. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 008-A: Euclid remains the Foundation's largest classification by volume. SCP-008 is unremarkable within that population and is not flagged for expedited review.
