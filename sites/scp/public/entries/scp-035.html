Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-035 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://chimehose.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Euclid-class object, personnel are advised that SCP-035's behavior is only partially predictable under repeat interaction; researchers should log anomalous outputs to the site's own Addendum before treating them as expected behavior.

Description

SCP-035 — designated "chimehose" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://chimehose.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-035 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

listen to the atproto firehose. every new post rings a bell, every deleted post plucks a string, and the pitch tracks how big the post is — the bigger it is, the deeper the note. a riff on Hatnote's Listen to Wikipedia, aimed at Bluesky instead, all synthesized live in your browser with Web Audio (real Karplus-Strong plucked strings for the deletions). @mfzx.net wondered aloud if it'd be neat with the firehose; norvid-studies tagged the bot to make it real.
