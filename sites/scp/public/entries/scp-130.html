Item #: SCP-130

Object Class: Safe  ·  Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-130 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://mootree.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-130 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-130, catalogued internally as "mootree," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://mootree.bisks.net/.

The clearest surviving account of the anomaly's function is its own front-page copy, reproduced below:

grow a family tree out of anyone's bluesky moots — no login needed to peek at one, sign in to grow your own. generations from account-creation age, birth order from the order they actually followed people in.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-130 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @ver.ooo. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 130-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-130 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
