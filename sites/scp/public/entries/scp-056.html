Item #: SCP-056

Object Class: Safe  ·  Temporal Digital

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-056 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://derivatives.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Safe-class object, SCP-056 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-056 — designated "derivatives" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://derivatives.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

a live pari-mutuel market on every bisks.net site: will this one get its own secondary market spun off it, guestbet-style? odds move in real time as people bet play money; markets resolve for real (no fake clock) the moment the bot actually ships one, detected by watching the repo itself.

Discovery Log

SCP-056 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @dave.9000ish.uk, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 056-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-056 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
