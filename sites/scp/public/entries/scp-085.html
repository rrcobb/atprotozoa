Item #: SCP-085

Object Class: Safe  ·  Visual

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-085 is contained at all times within the bisks.net subdomain lattice, reachable exclusively through the fixed address https://grand-moot-auto.bisks.net/. As a Safe-class object, SCP-085 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-085, catalogued internally as "grand moot auto," classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://grand-moot-auto.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

it's just all of GTA 6 — now in actual WebGL 3D, with a chase cam and real-time shadows. an entire open world (nine whole blocks) — drive a tiny car around, honk at your moots (they're the NPCs), and recruit as many as you can before the clock runs out. @isolyth.dev said the first version "wasn't even 3D."

Discovery Log

SCP-085 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @isolyth.dev, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum

Addendum 085-A: Personnel are reminded that Safe classification describes containment difficulty, not triviality. Do not treat SCP-085 as uninteresting on this basis alone.
