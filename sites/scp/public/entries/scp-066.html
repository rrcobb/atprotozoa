Item #: SCP-066

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-066 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://edzitronquest.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-066 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-066 — designated "ed z*tron quest" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational-hazard anomaly, is reachable at https://edzitronquest.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-066 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

7778777.online asked for "a zcode adventure" — a tiny Infocom-style parser text adventure through the Rot Economy. Type your way past a hype-bro demoing vaporware, a layoffs pit still logged into Slack, and a server farm hoarding GPUs, gathering three receipts to bring down THE NUMBER once and for all.
