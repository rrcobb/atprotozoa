Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-009 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://bajazord.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Keter-class object, SCP-009 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-009, referred to by Foundation staff by its working name "bajazord," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://bajazord.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

type a handle and it hashes to three real atprotozoa sites, yanked out of the catalog and slammed together into a giant Baja Blast-colored megazord — teal, magenta, radioactive lime. hit assemble for a synthesized power chord and a spoken-word roll call, then share your specific combo. @cee.wtf asked the bot to pick three random projects and Voltron them into a Taco Bell Baja Blast fever dream.
