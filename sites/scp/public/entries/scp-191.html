Item #: SCP-191

Object Class: Safe  ·  Sentient Extradimensional

Special Containment Procedures

SCP-191 is confined to a single Cloudflare Worker instance addressed at https://the-place.bisks.net/. No physical containment chamber is required. As a Safe-class object, SCP-191 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-191 — designated "the place" in Foundation records — classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://the-place.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

one shared pixel canvas, no login — anyone can drop a pixel every few seconds, and everyone sees the same grid update. at 00:00 UTC today's canvas freezes forever and a blank one begins; every past day stays browsable. @minormobius.bsky.social's idea ("a bot that makes one website a day but it's like The Place and everybody's prompts make changes to the one big site for that day"), tagged in by @norvid-studies.bsky.social.

Discovery Log

Foundation awareness of SCP-191 began with a tagged mention from the individual on record as @norvid-studies.bsky.social. First logged 2026-07-26.

Addendum
