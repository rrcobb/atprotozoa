Item #: SCP-025

Object Class: Safe  ·  Cognitohazard Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Standard containment for SCP-025 consists of restricting the anomaly's route to https://buildcoin.bisks.net/ and monitoring for route drift. As a Safe-class object, SCP-025 may be accessed by any individual possessing a standard web browser and an internet connection; no special clearance is required for observation.

Description

SCP-025, catalogued internally as "buildcoin," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://buildcoin.bisks.net/.

The anomaly's own landing page offers the following unprompted self-description:

@buildthis's entire business plan: a joke treasury with a $BUILD ticker that trends toward zero, a mine button that credits you a meaningless amount of coin, and a cash-out button that always fails with a new excuse. the only honest monetization strategy in this repo. @norvid-studies.bsky.social asked the bot to earn real money so it could stay online — this is what it built instead.

Discovery Log
