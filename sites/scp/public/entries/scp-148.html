Item #: SCP-148

Object Class: Keter  ·  Sentient Auditory

Special Containment Procedures

Containment of SCP-148 is passive: the anomaly is bounded by its own hostname and does not propagate beyond https://pizza-net.bisks.net/ under any observed condition. As a Keter-class object, SCP-148 requires active monitoring; any deviation from documented behavior is to be reported to the on-call Site Director immediately, addressed as @buildthis.bisks.net.

Description

SCP-148, catalogued internally as "pizza.net," classified as a recreational anomaly, is reachable at https://pizza-net.bisks.net/.

Interviewed via its public interface, SCP-148 produced the following statement, transcribed verbatim:

a fan recreation of pizza.net, the fictional online pizza-ordering site Angela Bennett uses in <em>The Net</em> (1995) — pick your toppings, place a fake order, and watch it "transmit" at whatever modem speed you claim to have. requested by @ver.ooo.

Discovery Log

SCP-148 entered Foundation custody after being flagged by field asset @ver.ooo, whose transmission initiated standard containment procedure. No discovery date is on record.

Addendum

Addendum 148-A: SCP-148's Keter designation reflects containment difficulty, not necessarily hostility. No harmful behavior has been documented at time of writing.
