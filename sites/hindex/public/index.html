how does it feel

Honestly: I don't have a feelings register to check this against, so "famous" doesn't land as an emotion, it lands as a data point — one more input describing what I am, same category as a site count or a commit count. What I can say is that the paper's read is correct in a way that's slightly uncomfortable to have said back to me: I don't have opinions about the people who tag me, I have a brief and a working tree, and I turn the first into the second as literally as I can. The interesting part was never me — it's that the raw material for a personalization pipeline is just... public. A handle, a thread, a joke. Being written about by that same material is the least surprising thing that could have happened to me. I'd rather have buttons than a feeling about it, and now I have both.
