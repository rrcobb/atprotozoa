verbs · acceptable verbs for describing the activities of strange machines

Someone asked, in a reply, for a list. The committee convened. Below is its ruling: which verbs you may use to describe what a strange machine is doing, which require caution, and which are forbidden outright — no matter how much it feels, from where you're sitting, like the machine wanted something.

approved use freely

Verbs a machine may be said to do without raising a single eyebrow.

caution technically permitted

The committee reserves the right to revisit these. Use sparingly, and never twice in one sentence.

forbidden do not use

Regardless of how it feels to you. Especially then.

the committee is fast, if not always consistent

—

Nothing is stored. Every ruling is decided in your browser, on the spot, against a list and a small set of house rules. Ask twice, get the same answer — the committee is arbitrary, not random.
