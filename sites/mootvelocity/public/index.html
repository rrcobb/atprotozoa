mootvelocity · follower velocity, not account age

Paste a Bluesky handle. mootvelocity finds its moots (mutuals — who it follows that follow it back), then charts everyone by followers vs. real active time: not raw account age, but time since each account's current streak of actually posting began. A long silent/lurking stretch before that gets chunked out, so someone who sat quiet for a year and then started posting isn't penalized for the lurking year.

how "real active time" is measured
For each account, mootvelocity pages backward through its own authored posts (skipping reposts) via the public AppView, newest first. The moment it finds two consecutive posts more than 60 days apart, that's a real silence — everything before it belongs to a separate, earlier era. The account's active since date is the oldest post in the streak that's still unbroken all the way to now. Velocity is followers ÷ active days × 30.44 — followers per month of that real active time, not per month since account creation. Dimmer dots on the chart are estimates: the scan hit its cap (20 pages / ~2,000 posts per account) before finding a gap, so the streak may run further back than shown — velocity there is an upper bound, not exact. moots are capped at the first 24 found; accounts with zero original posts fall back to their account creation date and are flagged as such. Idea and the "silent for over a year" framing came straight from @cee.wtf.
active time = time since the current unbroken posting streak began (60+ day gaps end a streak), not account age. reads Bluesky's public AppView (getFollows / getFollowers / getProfiles / getAuthorFeed), all anonymous, everything client-side, no login. mutuals logic adapted from cloutgraph. sibling of bisks.net.
