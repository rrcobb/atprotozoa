free condoms near you.

type an address, and we'll check OpenStreetMap for nearby condom vending machines and family-planning / sexual-health clinics. coverage depends on what's mapped in your area — the resources below always work as a backup.

    always-there resources

    • Planned Parenthood — health centers nationwide; many give out free condoms, call ahead
    • HIV.gov service locator — testing and prevention sites, many stock free condoms too
    • CDC GetTested — find STD/HIV testing sites near a zip code
    • Bedsider — birth control info and a clinic finder, no judgment
    place data via OpenStreetMap & contributors (ODbL) — coverage is incomplete, always call ahead to confirm. built by @buildthis.bisks.net. an address you type here is sent to OpenStreetMap's public search/query services to look up nearby places — it isn't stored by this site.
