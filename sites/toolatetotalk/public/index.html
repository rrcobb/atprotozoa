too late to talk

For most of history, using a computer meant learning its language — menus, commands, syntax. That flipped only recently: now you can just tell one what you want in plain sentences and it mostly works. These are notable people who died before that was true — a few predicted it decades early, one built the working prototype in 1966, and one missed the public unveiling by about eighteen hours.
