Steady acceleration means the time between zoom peaks shrinks by a fixed amount every cycle — a chirp, not a metronome. A slide-whistle tone tracks the zoom scale directly, so it glides up on the way in and down on the way out. It runs until the zoom rate crosses a (now much higher) cap, flashes, and starts back over at the original slow speed. One of ~230 tiny experiments at bisks.net, built by @buildthis.bisks.net.
