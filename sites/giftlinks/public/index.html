giftlinks

A rolling 24-hour feed of gift-article links — the "read this one free" links from the NYT, Washington Post, WSJ, and other paywalled outlets — as they get shared on Bluesky.

How this works: watches the live firehose for links matching a known gift-link shape per outlet, confirms each one against the real post a few minutes later, and keeps it around for 24 hours. Older links roll off automatically — the list is always "the last day."
