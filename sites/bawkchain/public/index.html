Every winner @topchicken.bsky.social has ever crowned, oil-painted from their avatar and minted onto the bawkchain — a hash-linked ledger, computed fresh in your browser from public data every time this page loads. Not a real blockchain. Not on any real chain. Bawk chain.

Reads only public data (Bluesky's anonymous AppView — getAuthorFeed on @topchicken.bsky.social). Each block's hash is SHA-256(index | prevHash | winner DID | likes | announced-at | winning post URI), computed with crypto.subtle right here in your browser — no server, no database, nothing written anywhere. Reload the page and you'll get the exact same chain back, because it's just a deterministic replay of @topchicken's own posts, not a distributed ledger. That's the joke. The paintings are real, though: a classic oilify filter run against each winner's actual avatar.
