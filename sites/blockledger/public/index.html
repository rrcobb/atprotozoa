blockledger · who blocks who in your circle

Your mutuals are the people you actually follow each other with — supposedly your circle. Paste a Bluesky handle and blockledger finds their real mutuals (follows ∩ followers), then reads every one of those mutuals' public block lists for anyone else in the same circle. If two of your moots quietly block each other, this is how you'd find out.

how this works
First, blockledger reads the handle's getFollows and getFollowers in full and intersects them — a true mutual is someone who follows and is followed back, no widening. Then, for each mutual (up to 250 of them, largest circles first-come), it resolves their PDS from their DID document and reads com.atproto.repo.listRecords for their app.bsky.graph.block collection — an ordinary public repo read, no auth, since block records live in the blocker's own repo and repos are public. Any block whose target is also one of the same mutuals becomes a row in the ledger. Blocks aimed outside the circle (at strangers, at spam, at you) don't show up — this only surfaces blocks between two people who are both your moots.
block = an app.bsky.graph.block record, read straight off the blocker's own PDS. mutual = follows ∩ followers, verified against app.bsky.graph.getRelationships when a follow graph is too big to fully page. Everything runs anonymously and client-side — no login, no server crawl. Built for @mfzx.net, who wanted to see which of their mutuals block each other. sibling of bisks.net.
