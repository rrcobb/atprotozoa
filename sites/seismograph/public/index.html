seismograph of the follows that don't follow back

Type a handle. We read who it follows and who follows it back, then run the difference — the one-way follows — across the drum. Each unrequited follow is one twitch of the needle; bigger accounts throw bigger spikes. It measures the quiet.

bisks & co. — model 1W “unrequited” — idle —
needle twitch = one account you follow that doesn't follow back spike height = how big that account is hover a spike to read who it is
Reads only Bluesky's public AppView (getFollows / getFollowers, anonymous). Nothing is stored; nobody is notified. Scans up to ~1500 each way. A quiet needle is a good sign.
