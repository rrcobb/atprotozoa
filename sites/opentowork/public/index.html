#opentowork

the linkedin ring, on your bluesky avatar. sign in with bluesky and it swaps your avatar for you — the original is kept in this browser so you can put it back with one click.

type a handle to preview
signing in is the approval: once you grant access, the ring is applied automatically. the grant covers only updating your profile record and uploading the image — it can't post or read anything.
