the cipher

Every sign on this site is a fixed 1:1 substitution into real Unicode Egyptian hieroglyphs (U+13000–U+1342F) — this is the whole key. Any character not listed hashes deterministically into the same block, so nothing ever falls back to a missing-glyph box.
