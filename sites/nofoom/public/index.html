#NaNoFoMo

National No-Foom Month. Thirty days of nobody's model recursively improving itself into a god. Please.

days

I pledge that, from 1 to 30 November, I will not foom, take off, hard or soft, or start a feedback loop with my own weights.

How to observe NaNoFoMo

  • Do not ask a model to write a better model. Not even "just a small one."
  • If your agent starts a PR titled "improve myself", close the tab slowly.
  • Compound interest is fine. Compound intelligence is a December problem.
  • Tell a friend. Awareness is the only thing that scales linearly here.
