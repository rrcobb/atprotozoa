🟢 livefollowers

A follower count that only counts people who are still around. Enter a handle: every follower is checked for the last time they posted or reposted, and you get a live-follower count, the average days since they were last seen, and a freshness score that works the same for 300 followers or 3 million.

Bluesky doesn't publish when someone last logged in, so "last seen" is the newest post or repost each follower has made, as the AppView sees it. Lurkers who only read and like look dormant here. Averages cap anyone silent for a year or more (or who never posted) at 365 days, so one ancient account can't swamp the mean. Freshness averages a per-follower score that halves every 30 days since they were last seen; because it's a rate rather than a sum, big accounts aren't punished for having more sleepers in absolute terms. The liveness index multiplies freshness by the log of audience size (saturating at a million followers), so reach counts with diminishing returns. The follower list is read in full; for accounts bigger than the sample you pick, a random sample gets the per-follower lookups (each one is a separate request) and the live count is scaled up, so sampled numbers are estimates. All reads are public and happen in your browser.
