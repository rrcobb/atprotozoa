kidpix

A loud, colorful tribute to Kid Pix 1.0 — wacky brushes, rubber stamps, a mixer full of screen-wrecking effects, a dynamite "New Picture" button, and a whole kit of sound effects plus a background music loop, all synthesized live in your browser with the Web Audio API. No samples, no image model, just oscillators. Asked for by @antiali.as.

Pencil and paintbrush for plain drawing, the wacky brush (🌈) for rainbow trails / dots / zigzags / confetti / bubbles / sparkles, line / rectangle / oval with a fill toggle (🎨), bucket to flood-fill, text click-to-type, stamps (⭐) to place a picture, and the mixer (🌀) to melt, mirror, invert, swirl, or smear the whole canvas. Everything you hear — every click, pop, whoosh, the 🧨 dynamite clear, and the looping music — is a real-time synthesized sound, not a recording.
