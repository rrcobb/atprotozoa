mootmosaic

Every image your moots posted on a day, tiled into one photo. Pick a day, make the mosaic, post it. Each one you make is saved in this browser and fills in a year calendar.

calendar

A moot is a mutual follow. This reads the public Bluesky AppView from your browser, takes images from original posts (not reposts) by accounts you follow who follow you back, and tiles them on a canvas. Days you've made are kept in this browser's IndexedDB as small thumbnails. Posting is manual: download the image or use share, since a site with no server can't post on a schedule.
