genepool

a population of 50 tiny robots, each born with a random string of moves. every generation, whichever ones get closest to the coins get to breed — their moves get spliced together and lightly mutated into the next batch. no robot ever learns anything mid-run. the population gets better, one generation at a time. draw walls, drop coins wherever you like, and watch it climb.
