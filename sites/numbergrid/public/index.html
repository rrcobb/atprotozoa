🔢 numbergrid

Every number you've ever spotted, kept as a bingo board that grows into the next perfect square as you add to it. Numbers live in your own PDS — sign in, add one, and the board is yours to share.

OAuth, scoped to only create net.bisks.numbergrid.number records in your repo — this site can't read your posts, follow anyone, or touch anything else in your account.

Reads are public: anyone's board (numbergrid.bisks.net/board/<handle>) is viewable by reading net.bisks.numbergrid.number records straight off their PDS, same as any other atproto record. Only adding a number needs you signed in; it writes one small record to your own repo, never anyone else's. The "smallest number you haven't seen" is the mex of your set — a nod to @ponder.ooo's question that kicked this off.
