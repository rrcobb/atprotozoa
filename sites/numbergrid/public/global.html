Reads are public and unauthenticated, same as the rest of numbergrid — nothing here is stored anywhere but each account's own PDS. Backfill prefers one com.atproto.sync.getRepo CAR download per account over paginating listRecords, so a big board doesn't cost more requests than a small one. "Most independently spotted" only counts numbers under 1,000,000 — nobody's going to coincidentally spot the same 10-digit phone number, so tracking that would just be memory spent on numbers that could never be hot.
