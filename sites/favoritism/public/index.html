favoritism

Paste two Bluesky handles. favoritism downloads each account's whole repo straight from their PDS — every like they've ever given, not just a recent sample — counts how many times each side liked the other's posts, and lays every one of those liked posts (with reply context, if it was a reply) out side by side.

built for @fromthewestmeadow.com, who wanted an actual breakdown of who's more generous with the likes — not vibes, the real counts, with receipts.

vs

reads each account's whole like history from their PDS, so prolific likers take a little longer. shows the most recent liked posts per side; the totals cover everything found — narrow it to a date range with the buttons above the results once they're in.
