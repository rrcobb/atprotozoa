threadwalk · walk the discourse map of your bit of the sky

Type your Bluesky handle. threadwalk finds your oomfs (your mutuals) and your oomfs-of-oomfs, samples what they've recently posted, and checks who liked it. Threads your people are into get placed near each other on a 2D map — closer means more of your people liked both. Then you just walk: arrow keys or WASD move a little dot around the map, and getting close to a thread opens it. The nearest thread from wherever you're standing is always something your people are also into.

reads Bluesky's public graph in your browser — no login, nothing uploaded. takes roughly 5–15s.

oomfs = mutuals (follows ∩ followers). the map re-scores every time you run it — discourse moves. sibling of mutualsky, dial a mutual and bisks.net.
