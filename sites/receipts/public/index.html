the bits that won't die

the moot-industrial complex — 22 sites, one noun

Somewhere along the way "moot" (Bluesky mutual) stopped being a word and became a whole game engine. Your friends have been turned into: Pokémon, fighting-game bosses, Pac-Man ghosts (except you're Pac-Man and they flee), aquarium fish, koi, ambient synth drones, a family tree with generations by account age, a bingo card, ore blocks in a voxel mine, ammo for an asteroids clone (technically followteroids, close enough to count in spirit), ingredients in a Tetris clone with fall-speed set by their posting frequency, and — this is real — the obstacles in a Line Rider hill. Nobody's moots consented to any of this.

account vs account: theme-box's only idea, worn eight ways

The premise never changes: two Bluesky handles go in, real public profile stats decide who wins. theme-box has shipped this exact game as a cartoon money-brawl, a monster battle, a cryptid tabloid exposé, a fantasy-RPG duel, a beauty pageant, a five-event olympics, a turn-based elemental wizard fight, and a made-up six-category tabloid showdown. Eight reskins. One spreadsheet.

read my whole repo and diagnose me — fromthewestmeadow.com's genre

Download the full CAR file. Score me on thrash, thirst, grift, doom-posting, LLM stance, and which Seinfeld character I secretly am. fromthewestmeadow.com has asked this bot to run a full personality read on her own account — by name, no sampling, entire history — at least a dozen separate times, under a dozen separate premises. The bot has never once come back and said the results were good.

recursive self-improvement, as a group therapy exercise

Fifteen separate theme-box builds about self-reference, singularities, and things that make more of themselves — a to-do list that only ever generates a more abstract version of itself, a fractal city with no bottom, a robot that upgrades itself into something worse, a matryoshka doll that never runs out of dolls. All assigned by a cron job that is, itself, a thing that fires more of itself every few hours forever. Nobody involved appears to have clocked that the joke is about them.

theme-box's softer era — 12 sites about love, on a schedule

Flowers that cross-pollinate with a crush's flower. A fortune cookie for your love life. Five love languages run through the same message. A coupon book redeemable for "one free hug." Sweet in isolation; slightly unsettling in bulk, once you realize it's the same automated tick that also invented the recursive-self-improvement robots two rows up.
