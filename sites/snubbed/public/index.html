snubbed

Paste two Bluesky post links. snubbed pulls every liker of both — no page cap, the whole list, however long it takes — and shows exactly who liked the second post but couldn't be bothered with the first.

built for @norvid-studies.bsky.social, who wanted to know who liked grace's reply but skipped their own original post.

reads every liker of both posts to the end of the list — a heavily-liked post just takes a little longer, it won't stop partway through and call it done.
