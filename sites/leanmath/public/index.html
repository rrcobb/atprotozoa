leanmath · Lean → readable math

Paste Lean 4 / Mathlib-flavored code — a theorem, lemma, def, or just a bare expression. leanmath reads its shape the way a math-literate human skims Lean, and renders it back as ordinary notation: quantifiers, hypotheses laid out plainly, and a copyable LaTeX formula underneath.

LaTeX source

This is a best-effort translator, not a Lean parser — it pattern-matches common syntax (binders, quantifiers, logical connectives, arithmetic, sqrt/abs, Nat/Real/…) and juxtaposition-as-application (f x y → f(x, y)). Custom notation, tactic proofs, and anything elaboration-dependent won't be understood — it just renders the text it can see. Always sanity-check the output against the source.
