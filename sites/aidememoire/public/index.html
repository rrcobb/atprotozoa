🗂️ Dept. of Retroactive Boundary Enforcement

aide memoire

Enter your handle and someone you've blocked. This pulls the real app.bsky.graph.block record, downloads what they were posting around that moment plus years of your own posts, cross-references the likes, replies, and reposts between the two accounts — surfacing the most recent ones and running them through the same classifier for a read on what they were actually about — and files a case explaining the block.

Not AI, not real psychology — a keyword-and-structure classifier running entirely in your browser over real, public post data (this site never calls a model of any kind). Blocks only, not mutes: mutes are private AppView state and this doesn't read them. Anyone who's ever seen your posts could already read them; this just makes the pattern-matching visible and a little theatrical about it.

works on any real, public block record — yours or (with their consent, ideally) someone else's.
