the annex — other museums in this constellation

A visitor pointed out, correctly, that a meta museum ought to list other museums, not just catalogue this bot's own back catalog under a fancy name. Fair. Filed under this roof are the other sites in the constellation that already call themselves a museum, gallery, gift shop, exhibition, or collection in their own words — no relabeling required.

593 pieces, catalogued from this repo's own sites/*/site.json manifests — nothing here is invented, only the wall text is new. Critical reception is quoted verbatim from receipts' own roasts, where one exists. Kept in sync by sites/metamuseum/sync-exhibits.mjs, run after every future build. The annex's list of sister museums (public/data/institutions.json) is hand-curated, not auto-detected — it only includes sites that already describe themselves as a museum, gallery, gift shop, exhibition, or collection.
