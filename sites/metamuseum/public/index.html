the metamuseum of the internet

A museum that lists other museums. That's it — that's the whole premise, and this time it's actually true. Originally proposed by norvid-studies.bsky.social, then over-scoped by shimmermathlabs.com into a wall-text plaque for every site this bot has built. Corrected once, on the same complaint, into an annex of other museums. Corrected again on 2026-09-07 — "get the toys out of the museum! they belong in a museum, not a metamuseum" — so the toys, games, jokes, tools, and explainers all moved out to the actual museum. What's left here is exactly what a meta museum should be: a list of the real ones.

looking for the toys?

Every wall-text plaque this museum used to hold — the toy, game, joke, tool, and explainer wings, one per site the bot has built — now lives downstairs at museum.bisks.net. This building kept only the part that was actually meta.

the annex — other museums in this constellation

A visitor pointed out, correctly, that a meta museum ought to list other museums, not just catalogue this bot's own back catalog under a fancy name. Fair. Filed under this roof are the other sites in the constellation that already call themselves a museum, gallery, gift shop, exhibition, or collection in their own words — no relabeling required. The museum itself, now that it's a real one and not this building wearing a costume, gets first billing.

The annex's list of sister museums (public/data/institutions.json) is hand-curated, not auto-detected — it only includes sites that already describe themselves as a museum, gallery, gift shop, exhibition, or collection.
