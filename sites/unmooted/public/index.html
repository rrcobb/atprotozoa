unmooted · catch who quietly stopped following

Type a handle and hit check. unmooted reads its public followers and follows right now and remembers the snapshot in your browser — no account, no server database. Come back later and check the same handle again: it diffs the two and calls out anyone who was a real moot (a mutual — followed back) and has since unfollowed. That's an unmooting. There's no firehose subscription and no global feed — just what you've personally caught by checking, twice, from this browser.
