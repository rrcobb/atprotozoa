    Nothing here is real mathematics — that's the whole site. P vs NP asks whether every problem whose solution can be *checked* in polynomial time can also be *solved* in polynomial time. It's been open since Cook and Levin's work in the early 1970s, it's one of the seven Clay Millennium Prize Problems (six remain unsolved), and the referee reports above cite three of the real reasons a proof has been so hard to find: the relativization barrier (Baker–Gill–Solovay, 1975), the natural proofs barrier (Razborov–Rudich, 1994), and the algebrization barrier (Aaronson–Wigderson, 2008).
