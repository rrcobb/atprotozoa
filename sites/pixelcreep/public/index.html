pixelcreep

Nobody notices a pfp change if it happens one pixel at a time. Upload your current pfp and the new one, pick the exact pixel it starts growing from, crop the new photo to a square, then drag the slider — 0% is a single pixel replaced, 100% is the new photo filling the whole frame. Three effects get you there: grow expands a circle outward from that pixel, zoom dollies the camera into it until it fills the frame, and original size grows the new photo's own patch in place with no camera zoom. Save any frame in between and set it as your pfp for a few days before anyone clocks it.
