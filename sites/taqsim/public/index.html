taqsim

A generative, Eno-sense taqsim: overlapping drone voices wandering through a maqam of your choosing, each one shaped by a semi-random attack/sustain/release so the texture never quite repeats, layered under an optional dumbek backing chosen at random the moment you press play. All synthesized live in your browser — nothing is a recording.

about the tuning

Maqamat here are approximated in 24-tone equal temperament — real performance tunes neutral seconds and thirds by ear, and they drift by region and by player. This is the standard simplification for computer-generated maqam music, not a claim of one true tuning.
