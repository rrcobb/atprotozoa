œuvre

the style of one account, the substance of another

Each account's whole repo is downloaded straight from its PDS. From the style account it keeps the skeleton of every post — the little words, the casing, the punctuation, the emoji, the line breaks. From the substance account it takes the subjects they actually write about, weighted by how often they come back to them. Then it stitches the two into a collected works.

Public reads only. Nothing is written anywhere, and nothing is generated by a model — it is a very literal mad-lib. Big accounts take a moment.
