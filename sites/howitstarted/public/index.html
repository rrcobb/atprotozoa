how it's going

Reads only public posts (Bluesky's anonymous AppView, getAuthorFeed). "Most-liked" is the highest like-count post the feed returns; "most recent" is the newest original post. Replies and reposts are skipped. Nothing is posted or changed — it just reads. The shatter is pure CSS; no post is harmed.
