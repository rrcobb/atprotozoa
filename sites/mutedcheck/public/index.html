mutedcheck

Sign in with Bluesky to load your muted words, then paste a post URL — mutedcheck tells you which of them (if any) show up in it.

mutedcheck asks for exactly one read-only permission (app.bsky.actor.getPreferences) to read your muted-words list, and never writes anything. Your muted words never leave your browser — the check runs entirely client-side.
