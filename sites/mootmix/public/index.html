mootmix your moots' music, one playlist

Your moots have been dropping songs in their posts for years. This downloads every moot's whole repo, pulls out every music link (YouTube, Spotify, SoundCloud, Apple Music, Mixcloud, Bandcamp…), dedupes them, and hands you one playable playlist.

Public reads only — no login, nothing written anywhere. Each moot's entire history is read, no page limit, up to 1000 moots deep. Give it a minute.
