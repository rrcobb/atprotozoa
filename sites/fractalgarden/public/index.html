fractal garden

click empty soil to plant a seed. it grows into a small recursive fractal plant. click a grown plant and it blooms — spawning one or two mutated copies of itself nearby, a generation deeper and a little stranger than its parent. every copy can be clicked again, and so can its copies. more of this type of thing, which themselves create more. the bed only holds so many plants, though — right-click (or long-press) one to prune it and make room.
