byok

A horoscope, read from your last few posts. Runs on your own API key, which stays in this tab.

Your key is kept in this browser's localStorage and sent only to Anthropic. This site is a static page on a Cloudflare Worker that never sees it — there's no server-side key and nothing to bill. The posts come from the public Bluesky AppView. Source: public/lib/byok.js.
