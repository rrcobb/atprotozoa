the lurkers

🦋 share to Bluesky

Reads only public data (Bluesky's anonymous AppView — getFollowers, getAuthorFeed, getLikes, getRepostedBy, getPostThread). Everything runs in your browser. A "lurker" is a follower who hasn't liked, reposted, or directly replied to any of the account's last 10 posts — checked against up to 2,000 followers (20 pages), so a huge account gets a sample, not an exhaustive count. The "say hi" button just opens Bluesky's own compose window with a friendly nudge pre-filled — nothing is posted automatically, and nothing here is written back to anyone's account.
