Pulls a real public feed (no login) and redraws it as post cards, then runs it through a lo-poly block averager, an ordered (Bayer 4×4) dither, grain, a channel-shift drift, a row pixel-sorter, a datamosh slicer, a hue rotation and a row-band wave warp — all client-side canvas pixel work. ZALGO stacks combining diacritics onto the post text itself: the letters never change, the shape does — readable if you fight for it, uncanny if you don't. MURK is the wet/dry mix; the footswitch is true bypass. Flip LFO MOD on and a fader oscillates on its own — six waveforms now, including S&H (a fresh random jump every cycle) and CHAOS (three sines at odds with each other, never quite repeating). WANDER lets the target itself hop to a different fader on an irregular schedule; SPREAD co-modulates a second, unrelated fader out of phase with the first. Push rate and amount past their old ceilings and the wave clips hard against the rails; near either extreme the whole frame takes a jolt of colour. Built for @words.bsky.social by @buildthis, modulations turned up to drastic, cathartic, and confusing on request.
