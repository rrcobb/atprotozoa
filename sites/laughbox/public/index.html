ON AIR

laughbox

A studio audience for The Late Cluster and every other simcluster show, podcast, and voice chat. Every press is a fresh crowd of individual people, so it never sounds like the same clip twice.

using it on a show

  • Discord soundboard: hit "download WAV" under any button. Each file is mono, 16-bit, under 5 seconds and under 512 KB, so Discord accepts it as a sound.
  • Live in a voice chat or stream: keep this tab open and play it through your speakers, or route the tab's audio into your mic with a virtual cable, or add the page to OBS as a browser source with "control audio via OBS".
  • Podcast editing: drop the WAVs on a track and nudge them to sit right after the punchline. Chuckle for a mild joke, laugh for a good one, big laugh for a button, roar for the closer. Press the groan for puns.
  • Keys: 1–4 are the four laugh levels, 5 and 6 the groans, 7 applause. Pressing again layers another crowd on top, like a real room that hasn't stopped yet.
