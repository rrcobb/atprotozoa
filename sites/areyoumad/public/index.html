areyoumad

Type a handle. It pulls up every reply you posted today, figures out who each reply was actually to, and checks whether they courtesy-liked it back. Anyone who didn't gets a card here — with a one-tap, pre-written post publicly asking if they're mad at you. Petty by design.

share this callout
Reads are all public, unauthenticated AppView calls — nothing is posted or liked on your behalf, and nothing you type is stored anywhere. "Courtesy-like" here means: someone you replied to today, liking that specific reply back. "Today" is your local calendar day. Only checked against people who follow you back (your moots) — a cold stranger not liking your reply isn't a crime. Scans are capped, so a very high-volume account may not get a fully exhaustive check.
