🚶 bigwalk

race your moots down a real track. alternate left/right to walk — fall too far behind and you're picked off.

We pull your moots (mutual follows — widened to plain follows if you don't have enough) straight off the public Bluesky graph, no login needed, and line everyone up at the start line. Anyone who joins can hit start — alternate L/R keys or tap the two foot buttons to walk. Send the link and they race live with you.
