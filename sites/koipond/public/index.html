🪷 A koi pond fed by the firehose

Every post on the live Bluesky firehose becomes a koi. The moment a post lands, its fish turns and darts — a flick of the tail, a ripple where it moved. So the whole pond is the shape of the conversation: quiet, then a sudden flurry.

The more an account posts, the bigger, fatter, and slower its koi gets. The chattiest handles on Bluesky end up as the great lazy carp drifting near the bottom; a one-post account is a quick little fry. No login, no server, nothing stored — just the real firehose, in water.

Hungry? Click the water (or hit feed) to scatter food — the fish will drift over and nose at it.
