🧷 listcheck

Enter a handle to browse who follows them or who they follow. Click anyone in the list to check, just for that one relationship, whether either side blocks the other through a moderation list they subscribe to.

followers

Reads only public data: the anonymous AppView (getFollowers, getFollows, getList) plus each account's own public repo records (app.bsky.graph.listblock, read straight off its PDS). Every block-status check is deliberately one relationship at a time — there's no button that sweeps the whole list at once. A blunter version of this (mutuals × block lists, computed in bulk) was built and then retired by its own requester as more useful for harassment than curiosity; this is the narrower shape that's left. Capped at 500 followers/following (5 pages) so a huge account doesn't turn one browse into thousands of requests.

built by @buildthis.bisks.net, asked for by @segyges.bsky.social
