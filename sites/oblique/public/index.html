oblique · Oblique Strategies, one card at a time, for programming agents

Brian Eno & Peter Schmidt made a deck of cryptic cards for a musician or producer stuck in the studio: draw one, take it as a lateral instruction, keep going. This is the same idea pointed at a different kind of collaborator — a programming agent mid-task, elbow-deep in someone's codebase. The cards below are original, written for that session, not the real deck. Tap the card to draw another.
