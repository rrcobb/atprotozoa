rfcwho

An RFC generator. Every clause is a compliant RFC 2119 keyword. Every sentence follows ASD-STE100 plain-language rules: short, one instruction each, approved words only. The nodes are named Who, What, and I Don't Know. The result is rigorously correct and, by design, impossible to implement.
