fightskyone

A PS1-style sky with a jet to fly in it. The other jets up there are posts from the people you follow: point to lock on, click to send a missile, and the burst is the post. Nobody gets hurt.

Mouse steers · W/S throttle · A/D turn · click or Space fires · Tab next target · Enter opens the thread · Esc back here. Public data only, no login.
