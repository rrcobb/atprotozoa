how does this work?

Each handle's public profile and last 100 posts (app.bsky.actor.getProfile + app.bsky.feed.getAuthorFeed) feed five criteria:

  • 🐱 most cat pictures — images whose alt text or caption is unmistakably about a cat
  • ✨ most creative bio — word variety, length, emoji flair, punctuation in the bio text
  • 🎭 wildest emoji rotation — how many different emoji show up in recent posts
  • #️⃣ hashtag hoarding — how many different hashtags show up in recent posts
  • 📏 longest-winded — average post length

Every score is read straight off real content — no follower-count formulas, no luck rolls, except to break an exact tie. Same two handles, same content, always the same result — a rematch is a replay, not a reroll, unless one of them has posted again since.
