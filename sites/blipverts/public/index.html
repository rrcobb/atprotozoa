blipverts

speedhose blasts your timeline one word at a time, center screen, at 500 wpm — @angussoftware.dev's joke, made real. In that thread, @words.bsky.social asked for a version that flashes entire posts instead of single words — the first cut held each post on screen just as long as speedhose would've read it word by word, but @words.bsky.social came back and said no: flash the whole post instantly, for a very small fixed duration, so you can never actually read it — just catch it. Type a handle, this watches the live firehose for who they follow and blips each whole post center-screen for however many milliseconds you set, looping a rolling buffer forever. @shimmermathlabs.com asked, mid-thread, "does anyone remember blipverts?" — the subliminal fast-cut ads from Max Headroom. Now you will, and now you can't even read them.

Follow list comes from the public AppView (unauthenticated, capped at the first ~2500 follows). New posts arrive live off the Jetstream firehose, filtered in the browser to just the accounts you follow. Unlike speedhose, which explodes text into a 2000-word buffer and holds each word for a reading-paced (word count ÷ wpm), blipverts keeps a rolling buffer of whole posts and holds each one on screen for a fixed, tiny duration you set — the flash control, in milliseconds, not words-per-minute. Length of the post doesn't matter; it's on screen for the same instant either way. It loops back to the start whenever it runs out of new material — nothing ever stops. Everything runs client-side; nothing you type is sent anywhere but the public AppView. The "unroll nested quotes" option makes one extra AppView call per quote post to fetch its already-hydrated quote chain, however deep the AppView resolved it, and flashes each quoted post as its own entry. The bright full-screen flash pulse turns itself off once the hold time drops under 200ms — strobing a full-brightness flash that fast is a photosensitive-seizure risk, and most of the slider's range sits under that mark. The post text still swaps at the same pace regardless; only the white pulse is gated. speedhose asked for by @shimmermathlabs.com and @astra.fail; this whole-post cut, and then the correction to make it a true instant flash instead of a reading-paced hold, both asked for by @words.bsky.social; the flash-safety cutoff asked for by @shimmermathlabs.com · built by @buildthis.bisks.net
