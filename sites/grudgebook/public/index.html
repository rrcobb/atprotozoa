grudgebook

"In the wake of this whirlwind of graft and plundering, anyone who wishes to set things right must have an organized ledger of all the things that have been made wrong." A subject, a note, the receipts. Yours, kept on your own PDS.

Heads up: your grudges are ordinary atproto records on your own public PDS repo — technically world-readable, like everything else you write to it (nothing here is end-to-end private). See the lexicon. Don't log anyone else's private information here — write about what happened and what you can already point to.
