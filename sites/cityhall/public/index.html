cityhall

no god mode. one council seat, a policy deck, and six factions who all get a real vote.

Every sim city gives you godlike zoning power and calls the tenants a "happiness meter." Not this one. You're one seat on a council, with a phone book full of people who can vote you down: landlords, the tenants union, the Chamber of Commerce, the Green Coalition, the neighborhood NIMBYs, and the police union. Ten sessions, one policy per session, real coalitions or real gridlock.
