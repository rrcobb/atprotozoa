how it works

  • peekaboo finds your moots — accounts that follow you and you follow back — via the public follow graph.
  • it pulls a handful of their recent posts you haven't liked yet, one per covered square.
  • tap a square to like that post for real (a genuine app.bsky.feed.like, straight to your own repo) — the square fades away and stays gone.
  • your reveal progress (how many of the 25 squares have lifted) is saved per-account in this browser. come back anytime to keep chipping away.
  • peekaboo can only create likes. it never unlikes, posts, follows, or touches anything else in your account.
