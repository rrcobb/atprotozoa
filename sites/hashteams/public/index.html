hashteams

Every account has a team, whether it's opted in or not: UTF-8(did) → SHA-256 → first two bytes, read big-endian → add one → a number from 1 to 65536. Enter a handle to compute it. The roster below only ever shows accounts that chose to join it — nobody gets listed without asking. Methodology from @mfzx.net.
