Duo in Amp Light

A dark cinder-block garage. Two builders share one drafting table: a stack of glass panels that leans in and welds rooms out of sparks, and a small magenta bot with an antenna and a torch doing the same from the other side. Little cubes with lit doorways rise off the table into a pulsing blue lattice. Amber amps on one wall, blue monitors on the other.
