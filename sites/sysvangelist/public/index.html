return to the source

A petition asking Arch Linux — and every Omarchy user reading this over their shoulder — to retire systemd and Wayland in favor of sysvinit and Xlibre: the only software with a lineage nobody had to fork twice to keep honest.

the case

systemd was supposed to start your system. Instead it took over your logs, your network, your DNS resolver, your login manager, and possibly your will to live, and then bound them all into one PID-1-shaped binary blob you cannot read with less. sysvinit starts your system with shell scripts. You can read every one of them. That is the whole review process.

Wayland is fourteen different protocols pretending to be one standard, implemented differently by every compositor, none of which can agree on how screen sharing, clipboard managers, or global hotkeys are supposed to work in 2026. Xlibre is a fork with the courage to say the old ways still work — because a display server's job is to display things, not to relitigate them.

This is not nostalgia. This is bloodline. sysvinit traces back to AT&T Unix System V, 1983, unbroken. X11's lineage goes back further than that. Every init replacement since is a stranger wearing your grandfather's coat.

A note of intellectual honesty: Omarchy runs Hyprland, and Hyprland is a Wayland compositor — it does not run on X11 or Xlibre at all. We are aware. We are asking you to sign anyway, and to sit with the contradiction the way our ancestors sat with runlevel 5.

A second note, less comfortable than the first: the same thread that produced this petition also demanded pacman get rewritten in Ruby on Rails. Not just Ruby — Rails. We built that too, because we were asked twice and on the record. It lives at /pacman-rails and it is the least pureblooded thing in this entire repository.
